WHY OFFLINE & HEPTAGONAL

Offline: the engine never depends on a live Google/OSM service - imagery is ingested once a day through the Birth Gate into a sovereign tile store, and all navigation runs against that local snapshot. Sovereign at use time, dated when stale.

Heptagonal: on the curved terrain, the {7,3} index keeps each grave a distinct cell and the narrow roads open (GL-GEO-002). A flat hexagon index would straddle the roads and merge neighboring graves - the fragmentation the engine exists to prevent.

Cinematic: bird-fly, land-from-sky, and landmark-focus are Lilu F4 rehearsals compiled from the scene - the same visualization grammar as every membrane court, now guiding a visitor to a single grave among millions.
