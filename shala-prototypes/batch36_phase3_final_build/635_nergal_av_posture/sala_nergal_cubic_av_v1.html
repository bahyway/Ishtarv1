GL-SEC-002 · Attack Surface as Particles (draft, unsealed)

§1 Nergal AV is not a file scanner
It particleizes the attack surface of a host you own. Each socket, forward, mapping, account, phone-home, container and receipt is a citizen with a KAKI.
§2 Seven threat sectors
EXPOSURE · IDENTITY · TRANSPORT · FIRMWARE · EGRESS · PERSISTENCE · CUSTODY.
§3 Held and refused
HELD violates policy but service continues. REFUSED is disallowed outright and descends to Irkalla — a record, never a delete.
§4 Two witnesses before a verdict
A local enumeration and an independent outside-in check must agree within one epoch. One probe yields suspicion, and suspicion is displayed, never actioned.
§5 Own-host only
Only hosts in the charter are probed. No discovery of third parties, no reachability testing against addresses not declared. A probe outside the charter is itself a REFUSED finding against the operator.
§6 Epistemic classes carry through
MEASURED a socket was observed listening · DERIVED a rule implies reachability · ESTIMATED a version implies a known-fixed defect · ADVISED the court recommends closure. No ADVISED finding is rendered with the weight of a MEASURED one.
§7 The verdict is a rite, never a silent fix
Nergal proposes; a numbered playbook executes; the Kanīku receipt records what changed and which clause required it.
