> *A version is not a number that increments; it is a reign that closes
> and a reign that opens. The registry never resets — it stratifies.*

## §1 — Name and Standing

A **palû** is a sealed era of the Ecosystem (v4.0, v4.1, v4.2, …), named per
NL-001 by a king's name. The **Palû Crossing** is the only lawful mechanism by
which one palû ends and the next begins. No component, stele, or announcement
may claim membership in a palû that has not been opened by this rite, nor may
any palû close without it.

## §2 — The Coordinator (Isimud Clause)

IsimudEngine presides over every crossing. Its two faces are duties:

- **The backward face** reads the closing palû: the Completion Stele, the
  census, the DRAFT ledger, the gate results.
- **The forward face** opens the next: the new era facet, the lineage
  confrontations, the stratified registry layer.

No other engine may perform a crossing. Nisaba may not coordinate; Gula may
not seal; agents may propose readiness but only Bahaa's run of the crossing
playbook is the word (CSR-08).

## §3 — The Completion Stele (WITNESS)

"Complete" is a particle, not a feeling. Before any crossing, a **Completion
Stele** is minted in EnkiMDB·7006 whose mandatory lines are:

| Line | Requirement | Witness |
|---|---|---|
| C-1 | All deployment gates green, including the 1B/1s law on target hardware | gate playbook Kanīkus |
| C-2 | TESTING_PLAYBOOK_PHASE1 Blocks A–F passed | test ledger |
| C-3 | Arsenal census closed — PB-321 sweep returns no unknown members | ARSENAL-INVENTORY report |
| C-4 | Every DRAFT tablet either SEALED or explicitly DEFERRED to a named future palû | tablet registry |
| C-5 | The Five Refusals affirmed for the closing palû | GX-COMMENCEMENT-001 lineage |
| C-6 | Seven-database journal health (7001–7007) — no open KISPU, no orphan quarantine | journal probes |

Every line carries its epistemic stamp (MEASURED/DERIVED/ESTIMATED/ADVISED)
and the sha-256 of the artifact that proves it. ESTIMATED or ADVISED lines
cannot satisfy C-1..C-6; only MEASURED or DERIVED convict.

## §4 — The Judgment (JUDGE)

Isimud reads the stele. **If any line is red, the crossing is REFUSED** —
with a signed refusal record naming the failing lines. Refusal over silence:
an era announced incomplete poisons every stele that follows it, because all
cross-era statistics would rest on a false boundary. A refused crossing may
be re-attempted only after the failing lines are re-witnessed.

## §5 — The Seven-Fold Seal (SEAL)

On judgment PASS, one witnessed sequence writes the **palû-boundary record**
into all seven journals (EnkiSDB·7001 → EnkiDDB·7007), KISPU-atomic in
spirit: either all seven boundary records exist, or none. The master **Palû
Stele** in EnkiMDB is the record of record, carrying:

- closing palû name and king; opening palû name and king;
- the Completion Stele's KAKI and sha;
- the boundary epoch T_palû;
- the seven journal receipt shas.

From T_palû forward, `era = <closing king>` is an **immutable
PHYSICS-predicable fact** on every particle of the closed reign. Time-travel
anchors are born here: `WHEN BEFORE EPOCH palu(Gudea)` means something
forever.

## §6 — The Herald (HERALD)

The public announcement is an **EMIT with a PROVE**: the completion
certificate is itself a NĀRU KAKI, and every public claim links to it. Nisaba
strikes the commemorative ṬUPŠARRU tablet — her sealed Stage-7 duty, nothing
more. No era unannounced without its proof; no proof unjournaled.

## §7 — The Opening (OPEN)

The new palû opens as **lineage, never mutation**:

- Every component crossing into the new reign is confronted by the **Kīma
  Labīrīšu rite** against its NUZI origin deposit, receiving one of the three
  colophons: **CONCORD**, **LAWFUL EVOLUTION**, or **SILENT DRIFT**. The new
  era opens knowing exactly which inheritances are still true.
- Components born in the new palû are lineage children with
  `BIRTH REGISTERED IN NUZI` and `era` stamped at the gate.
- The EnkiMDB registry **stratifies**: each palû is a sediment layer of the
  ziggurat. Cross-era statistics are two WINDOW clauses, never a migration.

## §8 — Scope Clause

This law governs the **registry's** transitions. What makes a change v4.1
versus v4.2 (semantic-versioning mechanics) is a smaller decree — a facet on
the boundary stele, decidable per-crossing by the Architect. The Palû Law
does not decide magnitude; it decides legitimacy.

## §9 — Law Tests

- **L-PAL-1** — no boundary record in fewer than seven journals (partial seal → the crossing is void and journaled as such).
- **L-PAL-2** — no crossing while any Completion line is ESTIMATED/ADVISED.
- **L-PAL-3** — no mutation of any closed-era particle; era facets are append-only strata.
- **L-PAL-4** — no coordinator but Isimud; no seal but Bahaa's run (CSR-08).
- **L-PAL-5** — the herald links its proof, or it does not speak.
