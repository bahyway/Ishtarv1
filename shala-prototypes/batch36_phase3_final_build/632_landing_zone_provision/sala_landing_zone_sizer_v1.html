Landing-Zone Sizer — how much archive does the arsenal actually need? 𒁾

This is a bench, not a Šala stage: there are no particles here, so there is no dome, no orbit and no catch — only an instrument and its gates. It answers one question honestly: given how many Copernicus products you land per week, how long you keep them, and what you keep after processing, how many terabytes do you need in three years — and is that a NAS purchase or a USB disk. The strongest lever is not compression: Sentinel products are already compressed, so zip buys single-digit percent. The strongest lever is what you refuse to keep.

DECISION BENCH · FIGURES ARE ESTIMATES · VERIFY PRICES AND DRIVE SIZES BEFORE BUYING
Honesty: product sizes are typical published ranges, not measurements of your AOI. Prices shown were current at build time and change constantly. This bench does not know your budget and is not financial advice — it computes capacity, not whether to spend.
