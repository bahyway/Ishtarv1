GL-FED-001 · Tablet of the Federation of BIGRINGs (draft, unsealed)

§1 No parent
Scales are not nested. Each scale of a host is a sovereign BIGRING with its own heart, RU spacing, sensor, epoch length and disclosure boundary. The host is the federation of its rings, not their root.
§2 Two kinds of leaf
Material leaves (oxygen, inflammation, pH, torque, battery, temperature) are host-type specific and end at the edge of that type. Structural leaves (Persistent-Homology band, Šību age, RU-ring order) are host-agnostic and run the whole federation. LamassuEngine mints the PH band per ring at that ring's radius sweep.
§3 Verdict rule
Two epochs from one ring is a suspicion. Two witnesses from two different rings piercing the same shell in the same epoch is a verdict, because two sensors cannot share a noise source. A cross-ring leak through a structural leaf that also crosses host type is the strongest witness in the system.
§4 SUSA and NUZI
SUSA carries the federation outward and holds each ring's boundary; NUZI keeps each ring's lineage inward. A genome ring may be fully sovereign while a gait ring is shared; nothing in the federation requires cutting a tree.
§5 Human and robot alike
A robot's rings are fleet · unit · joint · controller · firmware · instruction. Same dome, same shells, same court. Only the material leaves and the disclosure decree differ.
