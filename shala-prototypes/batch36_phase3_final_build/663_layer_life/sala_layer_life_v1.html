GL-LYF-001 · Tablet of Layer Life (draft, unsealed)

§1 The layer is the patient
A particle drifting is noise; a layer drifting is pathology, because a layer is an address. Every instrument reporting particle counts must also report the state of the layers holding them.
§2 Death is geometric
g = RU spread ÷ OU. At g ≥ 1.0 the shell has expanded to the tribe's girth: membership carries no information and the address has stopped addressing. Death is not a chosen threshold.
§3 Five states
LIVING · STRAINED (one signature) · DECAY (two signatures held two epochs, plus a measured rate) · FUZZY (the court cannot tell) · DEAD.
§4 DECAY and FUZZY are different illnesses
Decay is a trajectory with a rate and a predictable time of death. Fuzzy is epistemic — nothing is known to be wrong; the knowing is what is wrong. Treating them alike is the error this tablet forbids.
§5 The fuzzy deadline
Three epochs. Resolution requires a measurement, never a guess. Past the deadline the layer is DEAD by neglect and the record names the court, not the data, as the cause.
§6 Recovery is asymmetric
STRAINED → LIVING on one clean epoch. DECAY → LIVING needs two epochs of reversal. FUZZY → anything needs a measurement. DEAD does not recover.
§7 Death is recorded, never deleted
The dead layer keeps its final g, rate, witnesses and epoch. Its orphaned citizens are counted and must be re-addressed by decree; silent re-homing is forbidden.
§8 What instruments must publish
Beside every particle count: layers LIVING/STRAINED/DECAY/FUZZY/DEAD, fuzzy deadlines expiring within one epoch, and projected deaths at current rate — the last as ESTIMATED, outlined, never as fact.
