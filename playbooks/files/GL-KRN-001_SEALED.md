## §0 Why this tablet exists
Four scenes are not applications. They are the **kernel** — the places where the ecosystem shows what it is:

1. **HyperGraph · OntoGraph** — what relates to what, and at what arity.
2. **Storage · the seven EnkiDB types** — where the particles physically live and how their layers fare.
3. **Gates** — what is admitted, refused or held, and on whose witness.
4. **ETL Stations** — what moves, from where to where, and by which decree.

They differ in subject and in nothing else. This tablet fixes the **one pattern** they share, so that a steward who
has learned one kernel scene has learned all four, and so that a stakeholder cannot be shown a different standard of
truth by changing tabs.

## §1 The Nine Instruments are mandatory, not stylistic
Every kernel scene carries the full Naṣāru suite (GL-VIZ-001): Particle StoryEngine · scroll-zoom · Landing-from-Space
· Bird-Flight · right-click rites menu · Šurinnu legend stele · court journal · N9 Cinematic Camera (SALA-STD-001-A1)
· Membrane Motion Doctrine. A kernel scene missing one of the nine is **not a kernel scene** and may not be published
as one.

## §2 The Šala shell
Menus and options live in a **collapsible left rail**. The canvas is **full-bleed**. The canvas **splits into panes**
when a sector, a layer or a particle must be examined beside its context, and **each pane keeps its own camera** while
the **selection is shared**. A thin strip above carries the honesty line; a thin strip below carries the verdict.
No panel may steal height from the stage.

## §3 Four strata, always in this order
`FEDERATION → TRIBE/NODE → LAYER/EDGE-SET → CITIZEN`
Every kernel scene must be navigable down this ladder and back up it. The subject of each rung differs by scene:

| scene | federation | tribe / node | layer / edge-set | citizen |
|-------|-----------|--------------|------------------|---------|
| HyperGraph · OntoGraph | the estate | a tribe or concept node | a hyperedge class (arity ≥ 3) | a member of the relation |
| Storage · seven types | the seven ports | a tribe in one type | an MLU shell / a storage sector | a particle, and its extents |
| Gates | the gate wall | one gate | a rule class it enforces | a decision and its witnesses |
| ETL Stations | the pipeline | one station | one transform stage | one moved particle and its decree |

## §4 Density is answered by field, never by decimation
Above the citizen threshold nothing is drawn per-particle and **nothing is thinned for looks** (GL-FLD-001 §1,
GL-VIZ-010 §4). Amplitude is density. Individual KAKIs appear only on **descent** into one bin, plane, gate or stage
(Masku, GL-FLD-001 §5). Thinning a crowd to make a legible picture deletes precisely the rare particle the
Never-Averaged Theorem protects, and is forbidden in all four scenes.

## §5 The render grammar states confidence
`solid` MEASURED · `hatched` DERIVED · `dashed outline` ESTIMATED · `grey cross` UNKNOWN.
A stakeholder must be able to tell what was measured and what was inferred **without reading a caption**. Two things
of different confidence may never be drawn the same way. Projections are outlined; they are never solid.

## §6 The unknown is drawn
Unreadable extents, unscanned gates, unlogged transforms and unresolved relations are rendered as UNKNOWN **and
counted in the totals**. Omitting an unknown to make a scene look complete is forbidden in every kernel scene.

## §7 Every relation and every state carries its class, or it is not published
No edge without an epistemic class. No state above its witnesses: one witness is a **suspicion**, two independent
witnesses are a **verdict** (GL-AGE-001). A kernel scene that renders a one-witness finding like a two-witness one is
inadmissible, whatever it is measuring.

## §8 Geometry follows the subject; units follow their definitions
`RU` and `MLU` appear where orbits and leaves are defined. They may **not** be painted onto a subject that has no
girth and no shells merely because the estate is fond of them. A kernel scene either uses a defined unit or uses none;
borrowed geometry that carries no meaning is a lie told in a pleasant accent.

## §9 A held subject is never shown alone
Catching anything lights its relation web by default (GL-FLD-001 §9) and **freezes the court** — advection and
auto-orbit stop, the held subject bounces in place, and Esc releases. Its StoryEngine is a **named rite** in the
right-click menu, not a side effect of clicking.

## §10 The account is a shape, not a row
A subject's account is delivered as a **simplicial complex with a transparency ledger**: β₀, β₁, every line's epistemic
class, `τ` as the share that is not MEASURED, and the sovereign HeptaScript that produced it.

BahyWay is an **Anti-SQL environment**. There is no SQL in the ecosystem — not as a fallback, not as an escape hatch,
not in a corner of one tool. The operations are `PRESENT · ORBIT · WITNESS · PROVE · SYNC · EMIT`, and they are the
whole vocabulary. A citizen is not a row; a tribe is not a table; a relation is not a join.
Where an instrument checks for SQL, it is performing an **absence proof**, not a search for something that might
legitimately be present: an occurrence is a **foreign body**, and the scene carrying it is quarantined, not tuned.

## §11 The scene proposes; a playbook executes
No kernel scene writes to a store. Acts become **decrees** validated against the transition matrix of their domain,
and a numbered playbook executes them and writes the Kanīku receipt (GL-STW-001 §2, GL-SEC-002 §7). This holds for
Gates and ETL Stations exactly as for the Steward Court: a gate may be **proposed** closed from the scene, never closed
by it.

## §12 There is no UPDATE, and the scene must show it
A correction is a **new particle citing the prior KAKI**; the prior remains exactly as written. Kernel scenes render
supersession as **lineage** — the old subject stays visible and marked, never overwritten and never removed. A scene
that shows only the current value is hiding the estate's central property.

## §13 Determinism and rehearsal
Every kernel scene is seeded (`mulberry32(3600)`) so the same inputs yield the same picture, honours
`prefers-reduced-motion`, and states plainly in its banner whether it is a **rehearsal** or measured. An unmeasured
scene labels every state `ESTIMATED` and says so on its face.

## §14 The four gates of a kernel scene
`COMPILE` — it parses and seeds. `RUNTIME` — click, contextmenu and wheel are dispatched and answered.
`ORBIT` — every subject has a unique address. `CLASS` — every edge and state carries its epistemic class.
A kernel scene ships only with all four green, and shows them in its rail.

## §15 Admission of new kernel scenes
The kernel is closed to the four named in §0. A fifth is admitted only by amendment `GL-KRN-001-A1…`, and only if it
satisfies §1–§14 without exception. Ordinary scenes may borrow this pattern freely; they may not call themselves kernel.

## §16 Amendment
Amendments are numbered `GL-KRN-001-A1`, `-A2`, … and require a fresh CSR-08 rite.
**§4, §5, §6 and §11 may not be amended** — they are the honesty guarantees of the kernel: no decimation,
confidence visible, the unknown drawn, and the scene never writes.
