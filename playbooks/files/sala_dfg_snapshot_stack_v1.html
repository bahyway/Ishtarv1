A dome shows a state. It cannot show a journey, and at a billion particles its membranes overlap until nothing can be read. So the layers are laid out along a horizontal axis and each membrane gets a plane of its own: occlusion becomes ordering. In flow the axis is the pipeline — EnkiSDB·7001 → EnkiODB·7002 → EnkiQDB·7003 → EnkiDB·7004 → EnkiDW·7005 → EnkiMDB·7006 → EnkiDDB·7007 — and one tribe's population is snapshotted in each store it passes through, so a particle's life is a line you can follow. In layers the axis becomes the seven MLU shells of a single store. Every plane carries the same three units: PU as the σ of its density, RU as its ring spacing, MLU as its depth. And the density is answered honestly: above the citizen threshold nothing is drawn particle-by-particle — each plane renders as a field, amplitude is density, and individual KAKIs are recovered only by descent into a plane (GL-FLD-001 §1, §5).

GL-VIZ-010 · Tablet of the Snapshot Stack (draft, unsealed)

§1 One plane per membrane
Above the legibility threshold, membranes are never superimposed. Each layer is snapshotted onto its own plane along a flow axis, so overlap becomes order. A dome renders state; a stack renders passage.
§2 The axis is a journey
In flow the axis is the seven EnkiDB types in pipeline order, and a particle's life is a left-to-right trajectory across the Pre-Golden planes into the Golden Store. In layers the axis is the seven MLU shells of one store. No other axis is admissible without a fresh clause.
§3 The three units travel
PU, RU and MLU are carried in all seven types, not only in the Golden Store. A plane without its units may not be drawn, because a snapshot with no address is a picture, not a measurement.
§4 Density is answered by field, not by decimation
Above the citizen threshold, no plane is drawn particle-by-particle and none is sampled down for looks. Each plane renders as a field where amplitude is density (GL-FLD-001 §1). Thinning a crowd to make a pretty picture is forbidden: it hides exactly the rare particle the Never-Averaged Theorem protects.
§5 Citizens only on descent
Individual KAKIs appear when the court descends into one plane and only there (Masku, GL-FLD-001 §5). The field is a view; the store is untouched.
§6 A trajectory is evidence, not decoration
A tracked KAKI's line across planes carries its state changes and the decree that caused each. A line that enters EnkiQDB·7003 must show the clause that sent it; an unexplained jump is a finding against the orchestrator, not a rendering artefact.
§7 Cross-tribe membranes
Where two tribes share a leaf, their planes are joined by an arc at the same MLU. The arc carries its epistemic class like any other edge, and an unclassed arc may not be published (PB-385 gate A1).
