GL-FLD-001 · Tablet of the Wave-Field Membrane (draft, unsealed)

§1 Amplitude is density
The membrane's height at (m, θ, r) is the count of particles scatter-added into that bin by a single ENLIL pass. It is not a rendering of density; it is the density. Cost scales with the grid, not with N, which is how a BIGRING of 10⁹ becomes one surface.
§2 PU per shell
PU_m is the σ of shell m's density field — a true integral over the field, answering how much structure that layer carries. The shells sum to the mixed membrane; where they do not, the remainder is the cross-term band and is displayed as its own quantity, never folded silently into the components. Superposition is an assumption, and a body is not linear.
§3 Phase names the cause
Amplitude says a layer is loud; phase says which layers are driven together. Two shells whose dominant angular mode is phase-locked across two epochs share a cause; coincident magnitude alone does not. The verdict cites the locked shells, their phase and their RU wavelength.
§4 Two instruments, never one
The field finds the crowd's shape and the residual. The ember chamber finds the rare — a hundred particles in a billion that no average can hold. Neither may stand alone: a quiet field with hot embers is not a quiet organism.
§5 Field is a view (Masku)
The field is a mask over sovereign particles and is never the store. Descent through a bin re-runs the generator and returns the citizens' KAKIs with their biographies; identity is recoverable at every point of the surface, or the surface is not admissible.
§6 Ambiguous is not incoherent
A reading on a band edge is AMBIGUOUS — graded membership, geometry sound. A layer whose phase scatters between epochs is INCOHERENT — verdict withheld. Coherence is its own epistemic class beside MEASURED / DERIVED / ESTIMATED / ADVISED; incoherence must never borrow the credibility of ambiguity.
§7 Residual across organisms
Two point clouds of 10⁹ have no correspondence and cannot be compared. Two fields subtract. The residual against the federation median is the abnormality, at every RU and every shell at once, for the price of one subtraction.
