# GL-IMM-001 · Tablet of the Shared Membrane (draft, unsealed)

---

## §0 What is shared, and what is not
Clients are sovereign BIGRINGs and remain so. What federates is not their data but the **shapes their data takes**.

> **Antibodies are patterns, not tissue.**

A client that has met a pathology may warn every client carrying the same shape — and may do so **without a single
particle, count, name, tribe or KAKI leaving the ring it was born in**. The sharing is logical. It is never physical.

## §1 The shape family
Templates cluster by the identity already defined in GL-BND-001 §6:
- **exact** — equal canonical digest. `MEASURED`. One shape, however many names it wears.
- **near** — structural signature `(mode, unit-set, strata-set, β₀, β₁)` within threshold. `DERIVED`, and therefore
  a steward's matter, never the watcher's.

A **family** is the set of orbits, across any tribe, engine or client, whose template belongs to that cluster. The
family is the shell; the clients remain the rings beneath it (GL-FED-001 §1: scales are never nested).

## §2 What may cross, and what may never
An **immune signature** is a pattern-shaped object and nothing else:

| may cross | may never cross |
|-----------|-----------------|
| the canonical digest of the shape | any particle, KAKI or citizen |
| the geometry of the pathology — which shell, which phase relation, which direction `g` moved | any count, magnitude, population or rate |
| the clause that fired (`GL-LYF-001 §2`, `GL-SEG-001 §8`, a gate id) | any tribe name, engine name, client name or field name |
| the ordinal distance in epochs between onset and refusal | any timestamp finer than an epoch |
| the measure that first moved | any figure attributable to one estate |

If a value could be traced to one estate, it is tissue, and tissue does not cross. Where a signature cannot be
expressed without such a value, **it is not published** — and the omission is recorded as `UNKNOWN` in the family's
own account (GL-AGT-001 §4), never quietly dropped.

## §3 Three rings or nothing
A signature is published to the family only when **k ≥ 3 distinct clients** have independently shown it. Below three,
one estate's incident would be attributable by elimination, and an attributable warning is a disclosure wearing a
white coat. The count `k` is published with the signature; the identities never are.

## §4 A foreign signature is DERIVED until it is met at home
A signature arriving from the family is `DERIVED` for the receiving client and **may not be acted upon as a finding**.
It may do exactly one thing: **ask the local watcher to look**. When the client's own instruments reproduce the
pathology on the client's own orbits, the finding becomes `MEASURED` — and it is theirs, cited to their own artifacts.

> Nobody acts on another estate's observation. They act on their own confirmation of it.

## §5 Consent runs both ways, and refusal costs only the benefit
A client chooses separately whether to contribute signatures and whether to receive them. Declining either is free: it
forfeits the warning, never the service, never the band, never the price. A benefit that could be withheld as leverage
would make consent a formality.

## §6 The confirmation does not identify the confirmer
When a client reproduces a signature locally, that fact returns to the family as an **increment to `k` and nothing
more**. The back-channel carries no more than the forward one.

## §7 Scenarios are composed, never generated
A simulation built on the family is **composed from sealed templates**: deterministic, reproducible, and traceable to
the KAKIs of the templates it used. A scenario invented by a model is not admissible — it would replace a proof with a
plausible sentence (GL-AGT-001 §8). The composer proposes; it does not conclude.

## §8 The compatibility algebra of a shared membrane
Two templates may occupy one membrane **only where they address the same space**: their unit sets and strata sets must
be compatible, and the shared axis must be defined for both. `accum` over `(PU, RU) → bin` may share a membrane with
`ring` over `(MLU, RU) → shell`, on the common `RU`. `ledger` over `claim` shares a membrane with neither, and no
amount of visual charm makes it fit — this is GL-KRN-001 §8 applied to composition: borrowed geometry that carries no
meaning is a lie told in a pleasant accent.

**A composition that cannot name its shared axis may not be drawn.**

## §9 Composed is not evidence
A composed scenario is `DERIVED` until it is run against real orbits and judged. The sealed judge decides as it always
did (GL-AGT-001 §9); the composer may not rank its own output into a verdict.

## §10 Every crossing is a decree, and every client may read their own
Each signature emitted on a client's behalf is a decree with a receipt, and the client may read **the complete list of
everything ever emitted from their ring** — the shapes, the clauses, the epochs, and the `k` at the time. An immune
system whose messages its own members cannot read is surveillance with a friendly name.

## §11 Withdrawal
A client may withdraw at any time. Signatures already published cannot be unpublished — three rings shared them and
`k` is not reversible — but the ring contributes nothing further, and the register records the withdrawal in plain
words, with its date.

## §12 Amendment
Amendments `GL-IMM-001-A1…` require a fresh CSR-08 rite.
**§2, §3, §4 and §7 may not be amended** — patterns not tissue, three rings or nothing, foreign is DERIVED until met
at home, and composed never generated. They are the four clauses that keep an immune system from becoming a pipe.
