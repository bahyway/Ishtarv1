## §1 No parent
Scales are **not nested**. Each scale of a host is a sovereign BIGRING with its own heart, RU spacing, sensor, epoch length and disclosure boundary.
The host is the **federation of its rings**, not their root.
A human host's rings: `organism · organ · tissue · cell · mitochondrion · genome`.
A robot host's rings: `fleet · unit · joint · controller · firmware · instruction`.

## §2 Two kinds of leaf
`Material` leaves — oxygen, inflammation, pH, torque, battery, temperature — are **host-type specific** and end at the edge of that type.
`Structural` leaves — the **Persistent-Homology band**, Šību age, RU-ring order, energy state — are **host-agnostic** and run the whole federation, because shape does not ask what the points are made of.
LamassuEngine mints the PH band per ring at that ring's own radius sweep.

## §3 Verdict rule
Two epochs from **one** ring is a **suspicion**.
Two witnesses from **two different** rings piercing the same shell in the same epoch is a **verdict**, because two sensors cannot share a noise source.
A cross-ring leak through a **structural** leaf that also crosses **host type** is the strongest witness in the system.

## §4 SUSA and NUZI
SUSA carries the federation outward and holds each ring's boundary.
NUZI keeps each ring's lineage inward.
A genome ring may be fully sovereign while a gait ring is shared; nothing in the federation requires cutting a tree, because there is no tree.

## §5 Host is not per se a human
A host is any body of orbits: a person, a prosthesis, a robot, a fleet, a pipeline, a cemetery.
The frame is unchanged across them. Only the material leaves and the disclosure decree differ.

## §6 Disclosure per ring
Human rings are shared at `RU ≥ k ∧ |MLU| ≤ m` with hearts held (GL-UNT-001 §3).
Robot rings may open `k=0, m=∞` by **fleet-owner decree**, recorded as a Kanīku receipt.
No ring's boundary may be inferred from another ring's boundary.

## §7 Suspicion, never diagnosis
This federation names **suspicion** and routes it with its biography to a competent authority — clinician, engineer, fleet owner.
It does not diagnose, and no clause of this tablet may be read as a medical or safety claim.
Epistemic classes MEASURED / DERIVED / ESTIMATED / ADVISED accompany every routed line, plus COHERENCE (GL-FLD-001 §6).
