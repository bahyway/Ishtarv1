BIGRING Relief v2 — Mixed layers between tribes 𒁾

The BIGRING is the great orbit drawn as a loop on the dome. Every tribe sits on the loop as a hill; its heart is the summit. Walking down from the summit is measured in RU — one ring-spacing of the tribe's radial density — so integer RU draws as a contour line. Rising above or sinking below the membrane is measured in MLU — one factor-leaf crossing — so leaves draw as translucent shells stacked over the surface. A thread that pierces two or more shells is a deep leak. Tribes are no longer sealed hills: their contours overlap where they neighbour on the ring, leaves run as continuous shared shells along the whole BIGRING, and some citizens are guests — born in one tribe but seated in a neighbour's contour and leaf. The disclosure boundary RU ≥ k, |MLU| ≤ m is what SUSA lets the public see; everything inside k or deeper than m dims to sovereign.

GL-UNT-001 · Tablet of the Four Units (draft, unsealed)

§0 Standing units
PU tribe's whitened orbit-radius σ. OU healthy girth G₀. Both unchanged.
§1 Radius Unit
RU is the peak-to-peak spacing of a tribe's radial density measured from its heart. A citizen's radius in RU has an integer part (its layer) and a fractional part (its seat inside the layer). RU is tribe-relative and dimensionless; 3.2 RU means the same in every tribe.
§2 Membrane Layer Unit
MLU is one factor-leaf crossing. A citizen's MLU relative to a reference leaf is the count of membranes between them; an OntoGraph edge's MLU is its piercing count. RU is radial, MLU is transverse; together they address every citizen and edge.
§3 Disclosure boundary
SUSA exposes RU ≥ k ∧ |MLU| ≤ m. A federation of BIGRINGs publishes the union of exposed shells, each in its own RU. The heart and the deep crossings never leave.
§4 Deep leak
An edge with MLU ≥ 2 is a deep leak and is WITNESSed across two epochs before a verdict fires (GL-AGE-001 two-witness rule).
§6 Mixed layers (v2)
Leaves are ecosystem-wide, not tribe-wide: the same pH shell runs along the whole BIGRING and every tribe's citizens orbit it. Neighbouring tribes' RU contours may overlap; a citizen seated inside a neighbour's contour is a guest and keeps its birth tribe in κ[4..5] while its seat is recorded in EAV. A thread joining citizens of two tribes is a cross-tribe leak and is WITNESSed by both tribes' courts.
§5 Radial-active factor (GL-ALG-003)
A factor is radial-active in a tribe when its leaf mixture differs between rings beyond a two-epoch-witnessed threshold; it is inert otherwise.
