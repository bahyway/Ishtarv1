## 2 · Why it is membranes, not trees or nests

- A **tree** has a privileged direction (root→leaf), rigid edges, dead geometry.
- **Nested envelopes** are still trees drawn as circles — fixed containers, a
  privileged inside/outside.
- A **Mašḫalu membrane** is a living surface: it *deforms* under its particles'
  gravity and its neighbours' pressure. It has no privileged direction; you may
  enter any membrane from any angle; every membrane contains sub-membranes and
  sits within super-membranes. Containment is **membrane-within-membrane held by
  elastic tension**, not parent→child branches.
- This is *why* structure is "earned by force, not assigned" (prior doctrine):
  the membrane's shape is the equilibrium of the forces — the particles pulling
  inward by gravity, the neighbours pressing. The membrane IS the force-field
  made into a surface.

## 3 · Particle-gravity and integrity (the monitoring physics)

The particles a membrane carries exert **gravity**; that gravity gives the
membrane its shape and tension. Therefore the membrane's *form* is a readout of
its *contents' health*:

- **Sound (GOLDEN):** even gravity → taut, smooth membrane; balanced in/out flux.
- **Degrading toward unknown:** uneven gravity → the membrane **trills** (flux
  oscillates; particles cross erratically, hesitate, re-enter). The trill is the
  time-derivative of net membrane flux showing high-frequency instability.
- **Aged / decayed / depleted / drifted-from-fact:** the membrane **sags, thins,
  deforms**; its collective ColourID drifts along the decay curve (GL-VIZ-004),
  and *deviation from the expected decay curve is itself an alarm* — a record
  that still says GOLDEN but no longer matches the world.

**Two-witness confirmation** (Shakkanakku spirit): integrity failure must show
both **physically** (flux trill) AND **topologically** (unstable β₁ via
LamassuEngine vineyard trajectories) before a membrane is judged abnormal — the
same two-witness rule AsalluhiEngine already uses.
