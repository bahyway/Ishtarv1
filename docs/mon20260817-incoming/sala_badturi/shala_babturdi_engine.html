WHY THIS IS LAWFUL (AND STRONGER)

The tempting move — "a Particle near the attack is contaminated" — is forbidden: it convicts the innocent by coincidence. The lawful move is cost-attribution to the client's outside stream: Client-B's refused data concentrates on the hardest spot and carries the malware signature, so it is measurably more expensive to defend against. Their reward score is not lowered by blame — it is not earned by measurement. That claim survives a dispute; an accusation would not.
