# GL-DB-001 — No False Authority Law
**Status:** SEALED (concept). FOUNDATIONAL — governs all seven
EnkiDB databases and every surface that shows a human a number
(optimizer, dashboards, MARDUK, Shakkanakku reports, the τ
meter, cost displays, health predictions). A peer of
GL-DST-001 and GL-DST-003. Implementation queued behind PB-160.

## The Wound It Heals
Mature RDBMS query planners (SQL Server, Oracle, PostgreSQL)
present extrapolated cost estimates — "estimated rows:
1,000", "cost: 4,812.33" — in the SAME typography as facts.
The estimate is computed from stale sampled statistics
through cardinality formulas assuming uniformity and
independence that real data never has. The gap between
"estimated rows: 1,000" and "actual rows: 4,000,000" is the
leading cause of catastrophic query plans. The dishonesty is
not the guessing (guessing is unavoidable) — it is HIDING
that it guessed, dressing a forecast in a measurement's
authority. BahyWay refuses this at the constitutional level.

## Clause 1 — The Core Prohibition
No EnkiDB surface may present an estimated, extrapolated, or
forecast value with the VISUAL AUTHORITY of a measured one.
The typography of certainty is reserved for things that are
certain.

## Clause 2 — Two Kinds of Number, Never Confused
(a) MEASUREMENT — computed from an actual cook or an actual
    twin run; reproducible (deterministic cook); carries its
    PROVENANCE (which run, which twin, when). May be shown
    plainly.
(b) ESTIMATE / HYPOTHESIS — a forecast not yet verified by
    simulation. MUST be labeled as an estimate and MUST carry
    ε (its uncertainty). May never be shown in the styling of
    a measurement.
The line between them is the ε line: a value with no measured
ε is an estimate and must say so.

## Clause 3 — Impact Is Never Shown on a Guess
The optimizer (GL-ANL-001) may hypothesize remedies freely,
but a hypothesis is shown WITHOUT an impact number — because
none has been measured. Impact appears ONLY after the
hypothesis is verified by simulation on the OTA twin. Guess
the cause; measure the consequence; never the reverse. Wrong
hypotheses are eliminated by measurement, not opinion. If no
in-house remedy verifies, EnkiMDB templates are searched, and
they too must simulate on the twin before their impact shows.

## Clause 4 — Why EnkiDB Can Keep This Promise
Unlike an RDBMS, EnkiDB need not guess: data IS particles with
position (counts are counted, not sampled); the cook is
deterministic (numbers are reproducible, not extrapolated);
relations are typed and directed with declared cardinality
(a CrossTribe join's fan-out is a sealed property, not an
estimate); and the append-only ledger never goes stale in the
dangerous way (no mutating table to re-sample). The refusal to
print unearned numbers is therefore ARCHITECTURALLY EARNED,
not merely aspirational.

## Clause 5 — Universal Scope
This law binds every number-bearing surface, not just the
optimizer: MARDUK dashboards, Shakkanakku stakeholder reports,
the τ transparency meter, SLA displays, drift surveys, health
predictions. Anywhere the ecosystem shows a human a quantity,
that quantity declares whether it was measured or estimated,
and estimates carry ε. A governance product's whole value is
that its numbers can be trusted; it may not commit the sin
that broke trust in the tools that came before.
