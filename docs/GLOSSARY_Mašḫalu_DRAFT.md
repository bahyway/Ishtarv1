## I · The membrane itself

**Mašḫalu** — the sealed name of the elastic-membrane foundation. From Akkadian
*mašḫalu*, the **sieve / strainer / permeable filtering surface**. A membrane is
a *living sieve*: it holds a tribe, filters what crosses, and betrays its
contents' health by how its surface bends. Already in use as "Mashalu membrane"
(AsalluhiEngine doctrine); elevated here to name the whole geometry.

**Elastic membrane** — a living surface in 7D Hepta Space that deforms under the
particle-gravity of what it holds and the pressure of neighbouring membranes.
Replaces "tree" and "nested envelope" entirely.

**Particle-gravity** — the pull each particle exerts, proportional to its
mass/PU, which shapes and tensions the membrane. Even gravity = taut membrane;
uneven gravity = deformation and trill.

**Dent** — the inward Gaussian deformation of the membrane mesh where particles
attach and are processed. "The dent is the processing load made visible"
(Layered Organism). Rendered as `r = r₀·taut(v)·(1−dent)`.

**taut(v)** — the baseline tension profile of the membrane surface of revolution
(a slightly taut cosh profile); the shape a healthy, unloaded membrane holds.

**Hepta projection** — the 3D shadow of a 7D membrane. What renders as a ring /
orbit / BIGRING is the projection; the membrane's true shape is 7-dimensional.

## II · The constraint layer (DŪRU, PH-004)

**DŪRU (𒂦)** — "the Walls of Revolution." The constraint-wall mathematics of the
membrane: walls as surfaces of revolution, sectors of law, not symmetric shapes.
"The wall is the equality; the law is the inequality."

**Constraint creep** — the bulge of a tolerance wall; curvature (mean-curvature
|H|) = scope. "When a wall bulges, it is enforcing something nobody declared."

**Arkû gate** — the one lawful aperture in the membrane where inward entry is
permitted; every crossing cuts a KAKI (an Event-KAKI).

**Wedge / sector regime** — the angular regime inside a tolerance band.

## III · Integrity monitoring (naṣāru watch)

**Membrane integrity** — the live readout of whether a membrane's particles are
sound. Read from tautness, trill, and colour drift; confirmed by two witnesses.

**Trill (gravity tremor)** — the oscillation of membrane flux when contents
degrade toward unknown; the time-derivative of net flux showing high-frequency
instability. Measured (EMA of crossings/sec), graded CALM / TRILLING / VIOLENT.

**Flux** — the in/out rate of particles crossing the membrane. Smooth & balanced
= healthy; erratic = trilling.

**Two-witness rule** — a membrane is judged abnormal only when the physical
witness (flux trill) AND the topological witness (unstable β₁ vineyard loops via
LamassuEngine) both agree.

**β₁ / vineyard trajectory** — persistent-homology loop count over the membrane's
particles; unstable births/deaths of loops = topological tremor (LamassuEngine).

**Colour decay curve** — the expected ColourID drift of an aging tribe
(FUZZY_AGED → FUZZY_DECAY → DEAD_EXPIRED). **Deviation from the curve** is the
alarm: a record still labelled GOLDEN that no longer matches fact.

**Collective colour (diagnostic stain)** — a membrane's aggregate hue, read like
histology staining; its drift diagnoses the tribe's health.

## IV · Forensic response (AsalluhiEngine, GL-FOR-001)

**AsalluhiEngine** — the Data Forensic Analysis Engine (DFA); the physician of
failed membranes. Named for Asalluhi, son of Enki, god of naming-before-expelling.

**Āšipu Calculus** — the cause-level forensics: evidence correlation, the C-xx
root-cause registry, two-witness confirmation, relapse prediction, prescription
minting. Only the āšipu unlocks permanent remediation.

**Asû Calculus** — the symptom-level triage: the R-xx symptom registry, the
corrupt/malware/aged identification rites, the fuzzy scoring court. May run alone
but never cures permanently.

**Analysis-to-Solution Law** — DETECT → PROVE → PREDICT → PRESCRIBE, never
skipped. Curing a symptom while the cause stands is forbidden (it relapses).

**C-xx / R-xx** — root-cause codes (C-01 SOURCE_FEED_POISON, C-02 TEMPLATE_DEFECT,
C-04 RIKSU_SEVERANCE…) vs symptom codes (R-01 checksum drift, R-02 missing EAV,
R-03 PASHIRU hue variance, R-04 orphan reference, R-05 stale temporal).

**PARZU advisory** — the quarantine verdict (kaki_role 0x03) routing a sick
cluster to EnkiQDB (7003), evidence-grade, prune-exempt while investigating.

**Namburbi** — "its dissolution"; the rite that heals a queued particle, restores
its Birth Root Shade, and re-ingests it at 7001 for honest re-evaluation.

**Steward Queue** — the FIFO of red (unknown) particles awaiting Namburbi;
carries a live counter and each particle's fall-count (repeat offenders visible).

**Recidivism rate** — fell-again / re-ingested; the steward's report card, and
the financial argument for cause-level cures.

**State-unit ledger** — counts every colour transition (root→red→root, re-ingest)
as compute-per-state-unit; feeds the invoice and GL-ORG-001 homeostasis.
