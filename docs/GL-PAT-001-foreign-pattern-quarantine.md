# GL-PAT-001 — Foreign Pattern Quarantine & Promotion Law
**Status:** SEALED (concept). Implementation queued behind PB-160.
**Owners:** the Pattern Arsenal (GL-TPL-001), the OTA/staging
orbits (eriduous-vdi clean room), the drift instrument
(GL-TPL-002), the MADANU court (GL-DST-003), the release
process (NL-001 king-named eras), and the Architect alone.

## Clause 1 — Native vs Foreign Patterns
A NATIVE pattern is mined from the ecosystem's own tribes
(the ticket flywheel, EnkiDW findings). A FOREIGN pattern
is externally derived — scouted from another tool's public
behavior (Dayyālu), imported, or otherwise not born of our
own particles. Foreign patterns carry an EAV provenance
attribute `origin = FOREIGN` and are treated with strictly
greater suspicion at every gate. Provenance is never erased.

## Clause 2 — The Vulnerability Named
A foreign pattern can change the physics of the orbits
(bond as a nucleus, relate to other patterns, shift a
calculus). An unwatched foreign graft therefore risks slow
DECAY: the arsenal's topology drifts, other patterns are
pulled out of true (pattern-relates-to-pattern compounds),
and because each step was "accepted," the degradation
wears the costume of legitimacy. This is a supply-chain
attack in ontological form — malicious epistemology, not
malicious code — and it is forbidden the silent path.

## Clause 3 — Strictest Gates
A foreign pattern faces the HARDEST form of every standing
gate, never a relaxed one:
- Gate G4 (Z3 · Lean4 · BahyWay-Algebra): must not break
  Hepta uniqueness, must not drive a Betti verdict to HOLD,
  must not violate append-only monotonicity.
- The minting rite (GL-TPL-001): DETECT → PROVE → JUDGE →
  MOLD, JUDGE being the Architect's named approval. An
  unapproved foreign pattern is a rumor, not a law.
- CSR-08: no foreign pattern self-installs, ever.

## Clause 4 — Quarantine by Construction (OTA only)
Even after G4 and CSR-08, a foreign pattern is admitted
ONLY to the OTA / staging orbits (the eriduous-vdi clean
room). There it may bond to TWIN engines and change the
REHEARSAL physics — never production. Production truth is
sealed against every foreign pattern by construction.

## Clause 5 — Watched Decay (the drift instrument, inward)
The GL-TPL-002 drift instrument is pointed at the foreign
pattern's EFFECT: β₀ of the OTA arsenal, the divergence
metric, and the Apsu meter are read over a WATCH PERIOD to
see whether the pattern strengthens the simulation or
decays it. Decay becomes visible and measurable in
quarantine — BEFORE it can touch a client. A pattern that
degrades the orbits is refused promotion and its provenance
decay is chronicled (StoryEngine) as a scout warning.

## Clause 6 — Promotion Only by Versioned Release
Promotion from OTA to production is NEVER a hot-graft. It
is a VERSIONED, SEALED RELEASE: a new canonical/arsenal
version with its own Ed25519 seal, full lineage to its OTA
origin, king-named era (NL-001), and the Architect's
decree. Production truth changes ONLY at release
boundaries — never continuously, never silently. The
Architect is the SOLE authority who walks a foreign
pattern across the threshold; no agent, no advisory layer,
no automated rite may do so (this is a supreme-authority
case of CSR-08, not delegable).

## Clause 7 — The Immune Mirror
This law mirrors GL-ORG-001's reflex/cortex ladder: native
patterns may be minted in place (reflex); foreign patterns
are quarantined until a conscious, versioned release
promotes them (cortex). Together they make the organism
neither rigid nor autoimmune — able to LEARN from the
outside world through a sealed airlock the Architect alone
commands. Self-audit corollary: the scout (Dayyālu) turned
inward on our own planned public surface measures what a
foreign scout could extract — over-exposure is tightened
before release, per the publish-interface-seal-engine
doctrine.
