## 1 · What a Mašḫalu membrane is

Forget trees. Forget nested boxes. In BahyWay, a category (a "tribe" — E. coli,
Clostridial, Bacillus) is a **living elastic membrane** floating in a
seven-dimensional space (Hepta Space). Think of a soap-film bubble, but one that:

- **holds** the particles of its tribe inside it,
- **deforms** — dents, sags, bulges — under the weight (gravity) of those
  particles and the push of neighbouring membranes,
- **filters** — it is a *sieve* (that is what *mašḫalu* means): lawful particles
  pass through its gate, others are refused.

What you see on screen as a ring or an orbit (a "BIGRING") is the **3D shadow** of
this 7D membrane. The true shape is 7-dimensional; the ring is its projection.

**Why this matters:** because the membrane deforms under its particles, *its shape
tells you the health of its contents without opening a single record.* A taut,
even membrane holds sound data. A sagging, trembling membrane is holding data
that has gone wrong.

## 3 · How to read a membrane's health (the watch)

Look at the membrane. Three signals, read by eye:

**(a) Tautness / dent.** Particles attach and dent the membrane inward — this is
normal *processing load made visible*. A little dent is healthy work. But a
membrane that stays deeply dented, sags, or thins is overloaded or losing
integrity.

**(b) Trill (the tremor).** A healthy membrane's in/out particle flux is smooth
and balanced. When its contents degrade toward "unknown," the flux starts to
**oscillate** — particles cross erratically, hesitate at the boundary, re-enter.
That oscillation is the **gravity trill**. It is measured (EMA of crossings/sec),
not guessed, and graded CALM / TRILLING / VIOLENT.

**(c) Colour drift.** The membrane's collective colour is a **diagnostic stain**
(like histology). As a tribe ages, its colour shifts along a known decay curve
(FUZZY_AGED → FUZZY_DECAY → DEAD_EXPIRED). The alarm is not just "it changed
colour" — it is **colour that deviates from the expected decay curve**: a tribe
that decays *wrong* is more suspicious than one that decays on schedule. This is
how you catch the subtle, dangerous failure — **a record that still says GOLDEN
but no longer matches the world.**

**The two-witness rule:** never declare a membrane sick on one signal alone. The
tremor (physical flux, signal b) AND the topological instability (unstable β₁
loops, via LamassuEngine) must *both* agree. One witness is a suspicion; two
witnesses is a verdict.

## 4 · What to do when a membrane sickens (the cure)

Follow the ladder. **Never skip a step** (this is sealed doctrine — the
Analysis-to-Solution Law):

1. **DETECT** — the two witnesses agree: the membrane is abnormal.
2. **PROVE** — AsalluhiEngine's *Āšipu* runs the diagnosis: correlate the
   evidence (lineage %, batch timelines), name the **root cause** (a C-xx code:
   template defect, feed poison, orphaned reference…), with confidence.
3. **PREDICT** — project relapse risk: ~85–97% if you drain symptoms only, ~3%
   after the cause is fixed. And the cost of each path (compute, ŠĀRU, invoice).
4. **PRESCRIBE** — mint a **cause-level** numbered playbook and execute it.

**The warning this manual must give:** curing symptoms is a trap. If you drain
the "unknown" particles to quarantine (EnkiQDB) but leave the root cause standing
— a poisoned feed, a defective template — the membrane calms for a moment, then
**relapses**: the same sickness eats the next generation of particles, and you've
spent six stations of compute to fail again, and the client's SLA quota is
drained twice. *Symptom-only cures are the expensive path.* The āšipu (cause) may
never be skipped for the asû (symptom).

## 5 · The rebirth (Namburbi)

A quarantined particle is not dead — it waits in the **Steward Queue** for the
**Namburbi** rite ("its dissolution" — the ceremony that undoes a portended
evil). When healed:

- it sheds its red,
- **regains its tribe's Birth Root Shade** (its original colour),
- re-ingests at station 7001 for honest re-evaluation.

If the cause was truly fixed, it passes all seven stations → **REBORN CLEAN**. If
only the symptom was patched, it falls again — sometimes at a *later* station,
which is not progress but a more expensive failure. Every colour transition
(root→red→root) is counted as a **state-unit** statistic feeding the invoice and
homeostasis.
