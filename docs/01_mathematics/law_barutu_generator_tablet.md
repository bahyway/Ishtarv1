### Preamble — Divination, Redeemed

The omen compendia were combinatorial lattices: systematic products of sign-spaces, run
exhaustively through possible and impossible cases alike. Their genius was generativity;
their flaw was that no reading could ever be wrong. BĀRÛTU keeps the lattice and repairs
the flaw: every generated problem is born already carrying its own instrument — a declared
Θ, a governing dial, a τ bound, and an A2S staircase that refuses prescription until the
world itself testifies. **The generator proposes; the staircase disposes.**

### Clause G-1 — The Omen Table (the Generative Product)

The problem-space of the ecosystem is the product of sealed axes:

> **P = Rhythm (Θ-shape, 7) × Deviation (7) × Register (URUK system) × Opacity Sin (7) × Bridge Status**

The primary face of this product is the **Omen Table**: a 7×7 lattice of Rhythm × Deviation,
each rhythm guarded by its dial S₁…S₇, each deviation a lawful class of breaking
(collapse · second tribe · komma detune · migrating void · rate-law break · lopsided surge ·
radial escape). Cells occupied by history (Enron, Dieselgate, Flint, the sick cat…) are the
table's calibration; **empty cells are its predictions.**

A hypothesis particle carries from birth: its lattice coordinates, its governing dial, its

### Clause G-3 — Rank (the Order of the Watch)

Hypothesis particles are ranked, not believed. Rank is a sealed scoring of coherence
(do the chosen axes form a physically sensible omen?), novelty (is the cell empty of
history?), and register weight (does the domain carry stakes?). Rank orders **attention**,
never action: a high rank buys a hypothesis a place on the watch-list and an instrument
assignment — nothing more.

### Clause G-4 — The Two Disciplines (the Anti-Fear-Machine Clauses)

1. **No prescription before promotion.** A hypothesis particle is UNSEALED and is forbidden
   from the PRESCRIBE rung — visually locked in every theater — until an instance is
   DETECTed in real streaming density and PROVEd by independent corroboration
   (NIPPUR 4.2). The generator may fill the world with watchful instruments; it may never
   fill it with alarms.
2. **The Void is Sacred in problem-space.** Cells where no coherent omen forms are declared
   voids, drawn as voids, and honored as voids. A generator that cannot leave cells empty
   is a paranoia engine; BĀRÛTU's emptiness is part of its testimony.

### Clause G-5 — Promotion and Demotion

On DETECT+PROVE of a real instance, the hypothesis particle is promoted: its cell in the
Omen Table becomes occupied, its divination trail is sealed beside its detection KAKI, and
the ecosystem records that **the table predicted before the world performed** — the
Mendeleev event, dated and certified. Hypotheses that age without detection are not deleted
(nothing is): they drift to the NUZI archive as arkû hypotheses, retrievable by the Gap-list
rite if the world later moves.
