## Clause 1 — Charter and Boundary
The Physics service is the ecosystem's single executor of
dynamics: numerical integration, force generators (gravity
to a nucleus, springs, drag, damping, orbital correction),
constraint solving (rods, cables, lattices), trajectory
computation, and phase-space tooling. It CONSUMES equations
sealed by GeoEngine and certified at Gate G4; it NEVER
defines physical law of its own. One place where time steps
forward correctly — so Enbilulu, Girra, Igigi, the GL-ORG-001
twins, and NabuEngine's citation gravity stop reimplementing
integrators separately.

## Clause 2 — The Two-Tier Physics Law
(a) PRESENTATION PHYSICS — stage-side, GPU, cosmetic, free
    to approximate because it asserts nothing: bounce and
    flight easing, the MADANU bench's calm lattice, spring
    auto-layout of the Shape Operator canvas (wires as
    springs), ETL flow animation. Game-grade integrators
    (semi-implicit Euler, Verlet) are lawful here.
(b) TRUTH PHYSICS — engine-side, certificate-bearing: twin
    trajectories, GOLDEN-basin re-entry tests, domain defect
    propagation. Certificate-grade integrators ONLY:
    adaptive step with computed error estimates.
The tiers never mix: stage integrators may not sign
certificates; certificate integrators are not spent on
decoration. Every emission is tagged with its tier.

## Clause 3 — The Epsilon Source Mandate
GL-ORG-001's Humility Clause requires epsilon to be a
NUMBER, not a mood. The truth tier's adaptive integrators
are the ecosystem's epsilon source: every trajectory
certificate carries its computed local/global error bound,
feeding the twin-license machinery directly. A trajectory
without an error bound is presentation, whatever it claims.

## Clause 4 — Scope Discipline
The Physics service computes dynamics and EMITS STATES.
It builds no UI, no banners, no Theater extensions — those
belong to ShoWEngine, the Theater surfaces, and the Girsu
GX registry, which CONSUME its outputs: force fields for
layout extensions, flow states for ETL animation, phase-
space trajectories for the GL-ORG-001 visualization
mandate, spring states for the steward's bench. An engine
that also builds UI is two engines wearing one name.

## Clause 5 — Shared Kernels, Two Faces
Force and integration kernels are authored once (GLSL/WGSL
compute) and consumed by both lawful faces — Godot
RenderingDevice on bare-metal Fedora and the egui/WGPU
sovereign side — the same one-core-two-faces law that
governs pdm-graph-core and the Buzu bivector.
