## Preamble

A risk tool that crosses into Business Intelligence needs one currency, or
every service speaks alone and directors compare apples to omens. The
industry's answer — the composite risk score — is an average, and an average
is a hiding place: the site that is "acceptable overall" while one
kindergarten sits inside the fire reach. This tablet mints the Ecosystem's
risk currency the other way around: born per particle, carrying its
epistemic passport, capped when it is only theory, composed by maximum so
the worst-served particle is always the headline, and audited against
reality forever. Risk becomes a unit the way length is a unit — except this
unit keeps a score of its own honesty.

## §1 — Name and Standing

The unit is **Puluḫtu (Pu)**, a bounded scalar 0–100. Every Ecosystem
service that speaks of risk — Šēdu, Bārûtu, Nimrud, Kulla, Igigi, Mīlu, the
Ṭēmu courts, the Civil Protection Calculus — MUST emit its risk findings in
Pu under this law. A risk figure without Pu standing is rhetoric, not a
finding.

## §2 — The Minting Quadruple (Pu is never born a scalar)

A Pu value is minted per **(particle, hazard, horizon)** from a declared
quadruple:

- **I — Impact ordinal (1–5):** the SEVERE/HIGH/MED/LOW ladder of the Šēdu
  dossiers, anchored per service by covenant tables (what a "5" means for a
  school, a main, a strut — declared, not felt);
- **L — Likelihood ordinal (1–5):** with its **basis named** — measured
  trend, modeled distribution, or event statistics; a likelihood without a
  named basis is inadmissible;
- **R — the epistemic register** inherited from its evidence
  (MEASURED > DERIVED > ESTIMATED > ADVISED > SIMULATED);
- **C — the class:** PRACTICAL (a witnessed local pathway from hazard to
  harm exists) or THEORETICAL (generic hazard, no local pathway).

The quadruple travels with the scalar forever; a Pu stripped of its
quadruple is void.

## §3 — The Matrix

The scalar is **pu = M[I, L]**, a declared 5×5 matrix into 0–100, monotone
non-decreasing in both axes, sealed per domain **before** judging. Domain
variants are lawful but each is a sealed tablet of its own; tuning a matrix
after the fact to rescue or doom a verdict is a corruption event.

## §4 — The Register Passport

Every displayed Pu shows its register. A SIMULATED Pu may inform a Šēdu
verdict; it may never masquerade upward (GL-SIM-001 §3), and no pipeline
may launder a Pu into a higher register than its weakest evidence.

## §5 — The Theoretical Cap

A THEORETICAL Pu is capped at a declared ceiling **below the lowest action
floor**. Theory may summon watchfulness and further witnessing; it may
never, alone, refuse or condition anything. A THEORETICAL Pu converts to
PRACTICAL only when a witnessed local pathway is minted — evidence, not
argument, performs the conversion.

## §6 — Composition by Maximum (the Never-Averaged axiom as arithmetic)

Across particles, hazards, and services:

> **Pu composes by MAXIMUM — never by sum, mean, or weighted blend.**

A site's Pu is its worst-served particle's Pu. A network's Pu is its worst
segment's Pu. Dashboards and reports MUST show: the **distribution** of Pu
across particles, the **maximum** with the particle named, and the
**count above each action floor**. A single blended index is forbidden
output. This clause is the Never-Averaged axiom (Napharu §4) made into the
unit's arithmetic, and it is the entire difference between this currency
and the industry's.

## §7 — Action Floors

Per service, declared in advance: the **WATCH floor**, the **CONDITION
floor**, and the **REFUSE floor** on the Pu scale. Crossing a floor is a
discrete verdict per Napharu — fired by the particle that crossed, named in
W5H2 form. Floors are never moved to rescue a portfolio.

## §8 — The Exchange Covenants

Conversion of Pu into money, priority, insurance terms, or scheduling is
performed only through **per-service exchange covenants** — declared tables,
sealed like floors, with every conversion journaled. Ad-hoc conversion in a
meeting or a slide is void; the covenant is the only lawful bureau de
change.

## §9 — The Calibration Obligation (the unit audits itself)

Every prophesied Pu is a falsifiable claim. The **RU Ledger** (the Šēdu
Ledger of GL-SIM-001 §9, generalized) records prophecy against witnessed
outcome per prediction, per service; reliability records (how often
high-Pu prophecies were vindicated, how often low-Pu ones failed quietly)
are maintained and are consultable within the corpus. A risk unit that
keeps no score of itself is a marketing number; Puluḫtu keeps score. When
calibration drifts, the lawful remedy is a superseding matrix tablet —
never silent retuning (§3).

## §10 — HeptaScript Standing

Pu lives in the Quality (EAV) dimension of the particle it judges, with its
quadruple as attributes. Lawful sentences thus become possible across the
whole Ecosystem for the first time:

```
WITNESS particles WHERE Pu > 60 AND register >= DERIVED
ORBIT BY service, hazard
PRESENT distribution, max, count_over(FLOOR.condition)
```

No blended index answers this; the particles answer it themselves.

## §11 — Proof Obligations (deposited open)

- **RU-PO-1** — Matrix lint: prove every deployed M is monotone and every
  service's covenant tables are complete (no unanchored ordinal).
- **RU-PO-2** — Composition invariance: adversarial pipeline test proving
  no path from minting to dashboard introduces summing or averaging.
- **RU-PO-3** — Calibration round-trip: on synthetic outcomes, the RU
  Ledger reproduces known reliability; drift triggers the superseding-
  matrix rite, never retuning.
- **RU-PO-4** — Cross-service coherence: one physical scenario judged
  independently by two services yields Pu within a declared tolerance, or
  the divergence is journaled as a finding about the covenants.

## §12 — Closing

The old scribes weighed silver with sealed stone weights so that no market
could argue about a shekel. This tablet does the same for dread: one
weight, sealed matrices, a passport of evidence, a cap on theory, the
maximum always spoken, and the weight itself re-weighed against reality
for as long as the corpus lives.

*One unit for every dread; no average shall hide a particle.*
