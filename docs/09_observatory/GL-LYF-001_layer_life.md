## §1 The layer is the patient, not the particle
A single particle drifting is noise. A **membrane layer** drifting is pathology, because a layer is an *address*:
it is what makes membership mean anything. The court therefore counts **layers in each state**, not only particles,
and every instrument that reports particle counts must also report the state of the layers that hold them.

## §2 Death has a definition, and it is geometric
A layer is `DEAD` when its RU spread reaches the tribe's girth `OU` — that is, when it no longer **binds** its citizens.
`g = RU_spread / OU`. At `g ≥ 1.0` membership on that layer carries no information: the shell has expanded to the
edge of the tribe and every particle is equally "on" it. Death is not a metaphor and not a threshold someone chose;
it is the point at which the address stops addressing.

## §3 The five states
| state | meaning | evidence required | drawn |
|-------|---------|-------------------|-------|
| `LIVING`  | binds its citizens; no signature fires | — | solid |
| `STRAINED`| one wave signature fires | 1 witness | solid, amber |
| `DECAY`   | two signatures hold across two epochs **and** `g` is rising at a measurable rate | 2 witnesses + a rate | solid, red |
| `FUZZY`   | the court **cannot tell** — phase incoherent, or the evidence itself is unreadable | any | **hatched / grey cross** |
| `DEAD`    | `g ≥ 1.0`, or a FUZZY deadline expired unresolved | 2 witnesses | outlined, basalt |

## §4 DECAY and FUZZY are not the same illness
`DECAY` is a **trajectory**: it has a direction and a rate, and therefore a predictable time of death `(1 − g) / rate`.
`FUZZY` is an **epistemic** state: nothing is known to be wrong; what is wrong is the knowing.
Treating them alike is the central error this tablet forbids. A decaying layer is a physics problem.
A fuzzy layer is a *measurement* problem, and no amount of watching will cure it.

## §5 The fuzzy deadline
Every layer entering `FUZZY` carries a **deadline of three epochs**. Resolution requires a *measurement*, never a guess:
a coherent phase across two epochs, or the unreadable evidence made readable. If the deadline passes unresolved,
the layer is declared `DEAD` **by neglect**, and the record says so in those words — the cause of death was the court,
not the data. A fuzzy layer that quietly ages into death without escalation is a failure of stewardship and is
reported as one.

## §6 Recovery is asymmetric
`STRAINED → LIVING` on one clean epoch. `DECAY → LIVING` requires **two** epochs of reversal in `g`, because a single
good epoch is indistinguishable from noise. `FUZZY → anything` requires a measurement. `DEAD` does not recover:
its citizens are re-addressed onto a living layer or become Unnamed, and the dead layer stays on the ledger.

## §7 Death is recorded, never deleted
A dead layer is retained with its final `g`, its rate, its witnesses, and the epoch it crossed. Its orphaned citizens
are counted and must be re-addressed by a decree; silently re-homing them is forbidden.

## §8 What the instruments must publish
Every stack, atlas or dashboard reporting a plane must publish, beside the particle count: how many of its layers are
`LIVING / STRAINED / DECAY / FUZZY / DEAD`, the number of fuzzy deadlines expiring within one epoch, and the projected
deaths at current rate — the last as `ESTIMATED`, drawn as outline, never as fact.
