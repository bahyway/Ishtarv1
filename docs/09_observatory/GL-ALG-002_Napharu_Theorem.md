## §1 — Name and Standing

This law is named the **Napharu Theorem**. It is the apex tablet of the
GL-ALG series. It does not replace any member calculus; it subsumes them as
corollaries. Append-only: superseded only by a later tablet, never edited.

## §2 — The Stage

All lawful BahyWay mathematics is performed on **Hepta Space**: the 7D
Riemannian manifold of KAKI dimensions (Identity, Belonging, Temporal,
Integrity, Quality, OrbitalPosition, ColourID), carrying the metric

> g = diag( H(P) weights )

with covariant derivative ∇ as chartered in the Nabû Calculus. By the Hepta
Space Uniqueness Law, every particle occupies a unique real-valued position on
this stage. No member calculus may claim a different manifold; domain-specific
structure enters only as **enrichment of the fibres** (see §9).

## §3 — The Deficit Functional

Every member calculus defines a **deficit functional**

> 𝒟_Θ : (state on Hepta Space, covenant Θ) → ℝ≥0

measuring the departure of the witnessed state from a lawful reference:
girth dilation against healthy girth G₀; King-plot residual κ; transparency
shortfall τ against the AWĪLUM template; centroid displacement δc; the Refuge
deficit; junction defect Φ_Enbi. The covenant Θ names the stakeholder frame
under which the reference is lawful. **𝒟_Θ = 0 means the covenant is kept;
𝒟_Θ > 0 measures how it is broken, in the units of its own witness.**

## §4 — The Verdict Schema

Judgment is discrete, never continuous, never averaged. Every member calculus
routes its deficit through a **threshold set** T = {t₁ < t₂ < …} into named
regimes:

> verdict(x) = regime_k  ⇔  t_k ≤ 𝒟_Θ(x) < t_{k+1}

Instances: ELASTIC / PLASTIC / RUPTURE at strain S ≥ 1; the five Event KAKIs
(BIRTH, INFLECTION, SHELL-CROSS, DECAY-ONSET, RUPTURE-WITNESS); Šību routing to
ŠĪBU-ELDER / NUZI-ARCHIVE / EnkiQDB-QUARANTINE. The **Never-Averaged Theorem**
(GL-ALG-001-A2) is hereby promoted from member to structural axiom: no verdict
may be produced by averaging deficits across particles, populations, or time.

## §5 — The Witness Condition

No verdict issues from one projection. Every threshold crossing must be
attested by **at least two independent witnesses** — two projections of the
state whose noise processes are not identical. Instances: the two-witness
two-epoch trigger of GL-AGE-001; the Two Witnesses of Place (GL-NIM-002);
Simmu Law with Kīnu Witness (GL-HSI-001). A calculus with a single witness is
a draft, not a law.

## §6 — The Fadam Floor

Every deficit functional is bounded below in **resolvability** by the Fadam
Floor ε: no verdict may be claimed on a deficit smaller than the humility
constant of its witnesses. Below ε the lawful verdict is always
**WITHHELD**, never a forced regime. The floor is a property of the
instruments and the covenant, declared in Θ, never tuned after the fact.

## §7 — The Triality Condition

Stage, Deficit, and Verdict are not independent choices. The triality
condition asserts:

> Given the covenant Θ, any two of {stage metric g, deficit 𝒟_Θ,
> threshold set T} determine the third up to gauge.

The metric weights fix what "distance from covenant" means; the threshold set
names where that distance becomes act; and conversely a lawful verdict schema
pulls back to constraints on g. This is the unifying claim: a member calculus
is not a free invention but a **section of one structure**.

## §8 — Theorem (Napharu)

> **Every lawful BahyWay judgment is the threshold-crossing of a deficit
> functional 𝒟_Θ over Hepta Space, attested by at least two independent
> witnesses, bounded below by the Fadam Floor ε, discrete and never averaged,
