## Preamble

The science of architected lattices — Voronoi, cubic, octet, Delaunay;
periodic and stochastic; bend-dominated and stretch-dominated — has built
magnificent bodies and left them anonymous. The mesh knows where every strut
is and does not know who any strut is: indices renumbered at every remesh, no
lineage, no birth epoch though the parts are printed layer by layer, no seal
against silent edit, no class dimension though the field's own deepest
distinction begs to be one. This tablet ends the anonymity. Kulla, who waited
pending in the registry, is chartered this day as the engine of named
lattices, and every brick of the new architecture becomes a citizen.

## §1 — Name and Standing

The engine is **Kulla** (god names engine, per NL-001). This law governs all
architected and stochastic lattices in the Ecosystem: their generation, their
printing, their judgment, their aging, and their regeneration. It binds any
lattice minted, imported, or witnessed by BahyWay instruments.

## §2 — The Citizen Decree (KAKI for every brick)

Every **node** and every **strut** of a lattice is a born particle with the
full seven dimensions:

- **Identity** — KAKI minted at generation or at first witness; never
  renumbered, never reused;
- **Belonging** — the generator tribe: cell family (Voronoi / cubic / octet /
  Delaunay / declared other), parent cell, and neighbor set;
- **Temporal** — the birth epoch: generation tick for virtual lattices,
  **print layer and machine-state epoch for manufactured ones** — the
  dimension the file formats of the field throw away;
- **Integrity** — Kanīku: sha256 of the element's defining record; a silently
  edited lattice is a detected lattice;
- **Quality (EAV)** — strut diameter, material, local defect record, local
  coordination Z, load class, and any witnessed measurement;
- **OrbitalPosition** — the 3D position and, for stochastic families, the
  generator coordinates (seed lineage included);
- **ColourID** — the mechanical class of §3.

A lattice whose elements lack the seven dimensions is, in this corpus, a
**crowd**, and crowds have no standing before Kulla's court.

## §3 — The ColourID Mechanical-Class Law

ColourID of a node is its **mechanical class**, decided by local coordination
judged against the declared rigidity criterion of its family (Maxwell
counting; the numeric thresholds — e.g., the classical 3D stretch-domination
benchmark — are declared per family in that family's admission, never assumed
silently):

- **STRETCH-DOMINATED** — coordination at or above the family's declared
  rigidity threshold: load borne axially; the stiff class;
- **BEND-DOMINATED** — below it: compliant, energy-absorbing;
- **TRANSITIONAL** — within the declared band around the threshold; a class,
  not an embarrassment.

Struts inherit class from their nodes by the declared rule. Thus the deepest
axis of lattice mechanics becomes a queryable dimension:
*WITNESS struts WHERE ColourID = BEND-DOMINATED AND Belonging = shell-tribe*
is a lawful sentence, and no anonymous mesh can answer it.

## §4 — The Generator Fingerprint (quality judged by geometry itself)

A stochastic lattice is a **realization of a declared generator law and
seed**. Therefore the Ṭēmu Law witness stands as quality gate: the realized
point-and-edge process is tested (intensity, pair correlation, alignment,
coordination distribution) against the declared law at declared floors. A
printed or imported part whose realization **violates its declared generator
law is REFUSED** — misprint, damage, or counterfeit detected not by feature
inspection but by violation of law. This clause inherits the Cemetery
Fingerprint of Ṭēmu §5.5 whole: geometry itself refuses the false brick as it
refuses the false grave.

## §5 — The Regeneration Clause (store the decree, not the shape)

A lawful stochastic lattice is stored as **generator law + seed + boundary
membrane (Masku)** — the decree — from which the lattice regenerates
deterministically on demand, CLASS DERIVED, at any resolution. Mesh exports
are projections for foreign tools, never the particle of record. The
NANSHE-ONTOGRAPH result extends from images to structures: the shape dies,
the decree lives.

## §6 — The Bārûtu Charter (fatigue prophecy over the lattice organ)

A lattice under load is an organ in three dimensions. Per strut: state s(t),
decay by load cycles and environment, uncertainty declared. The Bārûtu
verdicts apply verbatim: **SHELL-CROSS prophesied per strut** within the
declared horizon, two-witness two-epoch attestation, WITHHELD-DATE where the
data cannot carry a date. Additionally the **Cascade Clause**: upon a strut's
RUPTURE-WITNESS, the coordination Z of its endpoint nodes falls; any node
whose ColourID thereby changes class fires **CASCADE-WATCH** over its
Belonging neighborhood — the failure's future read through the tribe's
changed law, before the second strut goes.

## §7 — The Ṭēmu Charter (flow through the named lattice)

Lattices work as heat exchangers, scaffolds, filters: flow is their duty. The
twin witness applies verbatim: PIV/PTV of flow through the lattice as the
Flow witness; the strut/pore process statistics as the Law witness; fouling,
clogging, or degradation verdicted only when both testify, WATCH on one.

## §8 — Floors

statistic (the sample-size floor of Ṭēmu §3); Temporal cadence for fatigue

class band of §3. No floor is moved after the fact.
