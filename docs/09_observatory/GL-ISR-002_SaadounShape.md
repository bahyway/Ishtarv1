## §2 — The Inversion Doctrine

A perfectly symmetric scatterer history — the single clean sinusoid of pure
uniform single-axis rotation — is the **poorest witness**: it is blind along
the rotation axis and carries no depth. Asymmetry is the payment for
dimensionality. Therefore this law forbids the reflex of "motion error
correction" from erasing structured asymmetry before it is read: the drift of
the whole Tribe is removed (SaadounFocus, family order §7), but the
**relational asymmetries between scatterer curves are evidence**, and evidence
is never compensated away.

## §3 — First Witness: Harmonic Asymmetry (the positions)

A body in real motion — roll, pitch, yaw at distinct frequencies — has a
precessing effective rotation vector. Each scatterer's range/Doppler history
is then a superposition of harmonics at those frequencies, and the harmonic
content of the asymmetric curve encodes the scatterer's projection onto
multiple rotation planes: **one coordinate per resolved harmonic**. Joint
decomposition across the Tribe recovers full 3D positions.

The decomposition instrument is the **Nēberu Slicer** (GL-MRD-002), pointed at
the sky: harmonic decomposition of dense orbit histories is its sealed act;
no new instrument is chartered.

## §4 — Second Witness: Visibility Asymmetry (the hull)

A scatterer on the far side of the body is shadowed for part of each cycle;
its visibility window is asymmetric, and the joint pattern of
who-occludes-whom across aspect is shape information no point cloud contains.
Reading the visibility functions of all scatterers jointly performs
**silhouette carving**: the hull and its major concavities are carved from
the shadows.

**Unnamed Surface doctrine.** Aspect coverage is never total. Regions of the
hull never witnessed remain in the membrane as **Unnamed Surface** — present,
bounded, and honestly marked unwitnessed, in the spirit of the Unnamed
Particle doctrine of KAKIGIS. An Unnamed Surface is never interpolated into
false testimony; it may be named only by later witnessed epochs.

## §5 — Third Witness: Amplitude Asymmetry (the normals)

Brightness across aspect is anisotropic: a flat plate flashes when its normal
sweeps the line of sight; a dihedral answers over a wide asymmetric lobe; a
wire answers everywhere. The amplitude-versus-aspect curve of each scatterer
testifies to the **orientation of its surface element** and its scattering
mechanism. Positions (§3) + hull (§4) + normals (§5) = shape in the full
differential-geometric sense. Three independent witnesses, one surface —
the Two-Witness floor of GL-NIM-002 is exceeded by design.
