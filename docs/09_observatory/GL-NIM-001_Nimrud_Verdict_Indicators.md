## 1. The Epistemic Stamp Law — Clause N1

Every indicator carries one of four classes in its EAV, rendered visibly wherever shown:

| Class | Meaning | Render |
|---|---|---|
| MEASURED | The physical quantity itself, from a sovereign witness | emerald |
| DERIVED | Closed-form algebra over MEASURED values | gold |
| ESTIMATED | Inverted through frozen-constant physics; carries a band | lapis |
| ADVISED | Ranked hypotheses or decree-table output; never asserted | clay |

**An ESTIMATED or ADVISED value is never rendered as MEASURED.** Presenting an estimate
as a measurement is the dashboard's self-minted negative. ε is first-class: every
non-MEASURED indicator publishes its uncertainty beside its value.
