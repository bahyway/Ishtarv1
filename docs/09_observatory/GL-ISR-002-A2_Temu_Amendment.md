## Preamble

Two questions exhaust what can be known of motion on a membrane. The first —
*what is the velocity at each address?* — is answered by Particle Image
Velocimetry: a field of measurements, the Id rehearsal. The second — *what
kind of spatial world is this?* — is answered by stochastic geometry: the
statistical law of arrangement itself, the discipline Šību already practices.
A field without its law is numbers without shape; a law without its field is
shape without becoming. This amendment marries them: no regime, hazard, or
hidden structure is verdicted unless **the flow and the pattern testify
together**.

## §1 — Name and Standing

This amendment is named **Ṭēmu**: the decree, the fixed plan. It extends the
Alaa Doctrine's medium-independent frame with a second witness *class* —
where SaadounShape reads asymmetric response and Alaa raises it above media,
Ṭēmu adds that the *arrangement statistics of the tracer population* are a
lawful witness in their own right. It adds to its parents and removes
nothing.
