## Preamble

Radar time-series analysis solved its decorrelation problem by inverting the
question: do not measure everywhere — find the few who behave, prove the
siblings who belong together, and read the years through them. This amendment
receives that discipline into the Nanshe court for hyperspectral time series,
where the Kīnu Witness of the parent tablet was already waiting for its
network law. What transfers is selection logic, network adjustment, and
admission-by-test — never the interferometric phase mathematics, for
reflectance is not phase.

## §1 — Standing

This is the fifth amendment to GL-HSI-001. It adds and removes nothing from
A1–A4. It binds all Nanshe processing of spectral time series (two or more
epochs over the same ground) and charters the PSI+HSI fusion toward Nimrud.

## §2 — The Kīnu Network Selection Law (persistent spectra)

A pixel (or particle) is admitted to the **Kīnu Network** only by
**demonstrated temporal stability**: a declared stability statistic over its
spectral signature across at least N_kīnu epochs (N_kīnu and the stability
threshold declared before selection, per dataset). Kīnu status is never
assumed from land-cover class, calibration-panel proximity, or convenience.
The Kīnu Network is the trusted witness lattice of the scene — sparse,
proven, and re-verified each epoch: a Kīnu that drifts is demoted by the same
statistic that admitted it, and the demotion is journaled.

## §3 — Nuisance Field Removal (through the network, never through the scene)

Illumination drift, atmospheric water vapor, view-geometry effects, and
sensor degradation form the **nuisance field**: smooth in space, largely
random in epoch — the opposite signature of true surface change. The nuisance
field is estimated **on the Kīnu Network only** and interpolated between
Kīnu, then removed from all particles. The correction applied to each
particle is recorded in EAV as a calibration act with its magnitude — a
removal that is not journaled is a mutation, and mutations are forbidden.

## §4 — Tribe Admission by Homogeneity Verdict (the proven siblings)

Where no single pixel is stable — soils, fields, rubble — trust is earned by
family. Two particles become **siblings in Belonging** only when a formal
homogeneity test on their full spectral time histories cannot distinguish
them at a declared significance floor. Membership is therefore a **verdict**:
discrete, refusable, re-testable, and journaled. Clustering by convenience
heuristics is not admission; a Tribe formed without the test is a crowd, and
crowds have no standing in the court.

## §5 — The Squeeze (Tribe signature as DERIVED estimation)

For an admitted Tribe, the shared temporal signature is extracted by joint
estimation over all siblings, weighted by their demonstrated mutual coherence.
The output is classed **DERIVED** under the GL-NIM-001 epistemic ladder —
never MEASURED — and carries in EAV: the sibling count, the coherence
quality, and the significance floor of the admission test. A squeezed
signature presented as measurement is a false witness.

## §6 — The Estimation–Verdict Distinction (structural axiom)

This amendment seals the distinction the squeeze demands:

> **Pooling is lawful in estimation; pooling is forbidden in verdicts.**

Joint estimation over a proven-homogeneous Tribe recovers state and is
admissible as DERIVED. But every **verdict** — an Event KAKI (BIRTH,
INFLECTION, SHELL-CROSS, DECAY-ONSET, RUPTURE-WITNESS), a Simmu finding, a
quarantine routing — fires only by threshold crossing per particle or per
admitted Tribe, per Napharu §4, and is never produced by the averaging
itself. The Never-Averaged axiom stands untouched: this clause is its
precision, not its exception.

3. **Sovereignty clause:** implementation is pure Rust from the published
