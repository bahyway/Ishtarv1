## §1 — The Answer in One Clause

The reviewer is not a person and not a program: it is a **college** — six
seats, five of them deterministic machine-gates and one human. Like the
foreign pattern (required status checks + required reviewer + review
thread), but sovereign: every seat's verdict is reproducible, every
refusal names its reason, and the review thread is the NĀRU journal,
append-only forever.

## §2 — The Review Lifecycle IS the Trichotomy

Every proposal — a new ALGEBRA clause, a new vessel kind, a reification
pattern, a shelf enrollment — enters `algebra_schema` as a **FUZZY
particle** (the bifurcation edge: neither living law nor refuse). From
FUZZY there are exactly two exits:

> **FUZZY → GOLDEN** — all six seats pass, the King seals (CSR-08).
> **FUZZY → DEAD** — any seat refuses; the particle is archived (never
> deleted) with the refusing seat and its reason inscribed on it.

Nothing lingers FUZZY beyond the declared deliberation interval: an
undecided proposal auto-routes to DEAD as `LAPSED` — the language does
not accumulate maybes.

## §3 — The Six Seats

**SEAT I · KULLA — the Grammar Guardian** *(does it corrupt the tongue?)*
- Grammar-conservation proof: the parser regenerated with the proposal
  must remain conflict-free and unambiguous (mechanical check on the PEG).
- **Anti-SQL sweep** (the gate already forged): no foreign vocabulary,
  in keywords or in examples.
- **Orthogonality test:** a clause expressible by composing existing
  clauses is refused as a synonym — *no clause may be a second name for
  an old power.*

**SEAT II · ASKUPPU — the Numeric Examiner** *(is the mathematics true?)*
- **Golden-value round-trip:** the reified vessel must reproduce its
  mother-law's WITNESSED proofs numerically from the *generated* code
  (HSQ-PO-3 made a standing gate).
- Property-based trials over the declared BINDING domains (proptest).
- Dimensional coherence of every BINDING (units audited; a length bound
  to a time is refused before Z3 ever sees it).

**SEAT III · MUMMU — the Logic Examiner** *(is it sound?)*
- Z3 at G4: domain totality of every ŠIKNU, no unbound symbols,
  refusal paths proven reachable, PROVE list complete against the
  mother-law's PO ledger.

**SEAT IV · EṬEMMU — the Ghost Examiner** *(does it behave like its
mother?)*
- The Ghost Logarithm scores the vessel's outputs on rehearsal inputs
  against the shelf's expectation for its mother-law; statistically alien
  behavior (surprisal above the sealed ceiling) is refused as a
  changeling — right equation, wrong spirit.

**SEAT V · KANĪKU — the Ledger Examiner** *(is the lineage whole?)*
- source_sha verified against the sealed tablet; PO inheritance complete;
  ELDER-SOURCE sweep clean; the proposal's own Kanīku well-formed.

**SEAT VI · THE KING'S SEAL — the Architect** *(is it wanted?)*
- The only human seat. The five machine seats can only say *lawful*;
  only the King says *desirable*. The countersignature is CSR-08 itself:
  **running the review playbook's SEAL stage is the approval act** — no
  click-through, no silent merge. When collaborators are chartered
  (the academic road), additional human seats may be enrolled by
  amendment; the King's Seal remains final.

## §4 — Quorum and the Order of Sitting

The seats vote as **gates, unanimously — AND, never majority**. They sit
in the order above (cheap and structural before expensive and
behavioral); the first refusal ends the sitting and inscribes the DEAD
particle. A proposal may return reborn as a NEW FUZZY particle citing its
dead elder — the college remembers every argument it has already lost.

## §5 — The Complexity Budget (against complication)

Corruption is refused by Seats I–V; **complication** is refused by law:

- Each era (king-named, per NL-001) carries a **clause budget** declared
  at the era's opening; the era of Gudea opens with budget **7** (the
  house number).
- Exceeding the budget requires **one-in, one-out**: a new clause enters
  only by superseding an old one (the elder goes DEAD-honored, its
  vessels ELDER-SOURCE flagged).
- Every sealed clause carries a Ḫubullu gloss AND one worked example in
  its tablet; a clause that cannot be explained in three plain sentences
  is refused by Seat I as obscurantist.

## §6 — The NĀRU Thread (the review record)

Every sitting writes to the NĀRU journal: proposal KAKI, seats' verdicts
in order, reasons verbatim, timings, and the Seal or the death. The
thread is the corpus's memory of its own taste — future proposals are
auto-checked against past refusals (Seat I cites precedent).

## §7 — Proof Obligations

- **UMN-PO-1** — Refusal fidelity: each seat, fed a crafted violation
  (synonym clause; wrong golden value; unbound symbol; changeling
  behavior; broken sha), refuses with the correct named reason.
- **UMN-PO-2** — Lifecycle soundness: FUZZY particles reach exactly
  GOLDEN or DEAD; LAPSED fires at the interval; DEAD is never deleted.
- **UMN-PO-3** — Budget enforcement: the 8th clause of an era is refused
  without a supersession.
- **UMN-PO-4** — Determinism: two sittings on the same proposal yield
  byte-identical verdicts and reasons.

## §8 — Closing

The kings of Nineveh received no omen raw: the ummânu had read it against
the series, argued it among themselves, and signed. The reports survive
today with their signatures — the oldest review threads on earth. So now
the language: five examiners who cannot be flattered, one King who cannot
be bypassed, a journal that cannot forget — and a tongue that stays
sharp because every word that enters it was made to fight its way in.

*The seats vote as gates; the King seals; the NĀRU remembers.*
