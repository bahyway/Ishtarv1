## The Mandatory Instrument Suite

Every Šala HTML court ships with ALL of the following, unasked:

**N1 · STORYENGINE** — click any particle: its cumulative biography opens
in the story panel and grows with acts/campaigns. No mute citizens.

**N2 · ZOOM & BOUNCE** — scroll-zoom (with 1/√z compensation), drag
rotate/pan with 9px drag-guard, click-time nearest picking; the selected
particle BOUNCES (decaying |sin|), its tribe-siblings shimmer.

**N3 · LANDING-FROM-SPACE** — for any event or chosen particle, the camera
departs to high orbit and DESCENDS onto the target's address in one
continuous eased flight. Events in the Šurinnu stele carry their own
landing coordinates.

**N4 · BIRD-FLIGHT** — a toggling mode (key B) in which the camera glides
above the membrane like a bird: slow orbital drift + gentle altitude
breathing, interruptible by any user input.

**N5 · RITES MENU & WORKBENCH** — right-click any particle: 📖 story,
⊕ maximize, ⇱ Workbench; plus literal drag-to-dropzone. The Workbench
shows measured dependencies, Tribe, orbit spec, lineage, hyperedges.

**N6 · ŠURINNU STELE** — the compact register legend (GL-VIZ-002
grammar: hollow/filled/pulsing, one-line gloss footer), acting as a
CONTROLLER: isolate tribes, land on events. (Kudurru remains the sealed
name of KAKIGIS beacon landmarks — never of panels.)

**N7 · COURT JOURNAL & HONESTY** — prepend journal (UTC), red doctrine
banner, SIMULATED/rehearsal declarations where true, window.onerror fault
banner. The court never decorates noise and never hides a failure.

**N8 · THE GATES** — COMPILE GATE (syntax) and RUNTIME GATE (headless
harness driving the full user journey with domain assertions) pass before
any delivery. No gate, no court.

## Drop-in
`bahyway_nasaru_instruments.js` implements N2–N4 camera/selection
machinery + landing and bird-flight; `bahyway_surinnu_legend.js`
implements N6. Courts import both; only scene data and stories are
bespoke.
