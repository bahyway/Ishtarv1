## 1 · Each Tribe is a BIGRING

A Tribe (a root category / lineage) renders as a BIGRING — its own particle
structure, coloured in its **root colour-band** (GL-VIZ-004): every particle in
the tribe wears a distinct shade-degree of the tribe's hue. So a tribe reads as
a coherent coloured ring, and its members are individually distinguishable
within it by shade.

## 2 · CrossTribe-KAKI federates the rings

Because no two particles share a 7D Hepta post (Uniqueness Law), particles from
*different* tribes sitting in **very near posts** signal a real tribe relation.
**CrossTribe-KAKI** (GL-KAKI-002 §4) records these tribe-to-tribe relations and
locations. In the federation view they are the **connective structure between
rings** — they position the BIGRINGs relative to each other and draw the
between-tribe relationships.

- Federation layout is *earned from the data* (near-adjacency in 7D), not
  assigned — consistent with "position is earned by force, not assigned."

The federation is navigated by the colour system (GL-VIZ-004):

Ordinary knowledge-graph tools show one flat graph of typed nodes. BWVL's

particle) seen at a scale. The federation is not a bigger graph; it is the same
monist substance read at its widest.
