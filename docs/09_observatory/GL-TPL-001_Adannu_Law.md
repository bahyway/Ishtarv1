## Preamble

The remote-sensing industry treats a scene as timeless: one spot, one
classification, one map — and when two acquisitions of the same ground
disagree, it calls the difference error and averages it away. This tablet
seals the opposite understanding, native to Triple-O since its first word:
particles live, age, wet, dry, and decay; therefore the harmony of a spot
is a function of its **adannu** — its appointed time and conditions — and
the same Earth spot **lawfully owns many templates**, each sealed, each
true of its moment, all joined in one genealogy. What the industry calls
noise, this law calls biography.

## §1 — Name and Standing

The law is **Adannu**. It governs the identity, stamping, lineage, and
cross-temporal querying of every template born through the Transit Rite of
GL-CLR-001 §8, for every spectral source in the Ecosystem.

## §2 — The Template Doctrine

> **A template is not a place. A template is a place × its conditions.**

Every template carries, from birth, its **adannu-stamp**: acquisition time,
season, and the declared condition covariates of its moment (moisture
regime, phenology, disturbance state — per-domain covenant). A template
without an adannu-stamp is void. The stamp is set at S0, at harvest, before
any processing — the time of witness is itself witnessed.

## §3 — The Lineage Clause (NUZI genealogy of templates)

Templates of one spot form a **lineage**, kept in NUZI: each new template
names its predecessor; none overwrites another. Supersession is succession
— the drought template does not correct the spring template; it *follows*
it, and both remain true of their adannu forever. The lineage is the spot's
chronicle, and deleting an ancestor is a corruption event.

## §4 — The Root Persistence Clause

Across a lineage, **the roots persist**: the sealed molds of the spot's
Tribes are shared by every template in the lineage, immutable under KAKI
v4.0 (GL-CLR-001 §9). Conditions change abundances, sheds, dominances, and
the Unnamed census — never the roots themselves. A genuinely new root may
enter the lineage only by the lawful path: an Unnamed cohort's petition
from EnkiQDB, judged, cast by Nalbanu, and sealed as a successor casting
with its NUZI parentage recorded. The center of the wheel does not turn.

## §5 — The Many-Faces Theorem (and the Timeless-Scene Fallacy)

> **One spot lawfully owns many ontographs.**

Two templates of one spot that differ are not in contradiction; they are
two true sentences about two moments. The contrary assumption — that a
spot has one template and disagreement is error — is hereby named the
**Timeless-Scene Fallacy** and is inadmissible reasoning anywhere in the
Ecosystem. Averaging epochs to manufacture a single face is a violation of
the Never-Averaged axiom applied to time.

## §6 — Cross-Adannu Query Standing

The lineage is queryable as a first-class dimension. Lawful sentences:

```
WITNESS spot(u,v) ACROSS templates ORBIT BY adannu
PRESENT root, shed, unnamed          -- one particle's biography in time

WITNESS templates OF spot WHERE census.unnamed > floor
ORBIT BY adannu                      -- when did this ground stop being
                                     -- expressible?
```

A query that names a place without naming a template or an adannu range is
answered with the lineage, never with a silent "latest".

## §7 — Change as Events (template subtraction)

The difference between successive templates in a lineage is not a report;
it is a set of **Events** per the Nanshe canon: a Tribe's shed distribution
drifting fires INFLECTION; a dominance flip is a SHELL-CROSS of identity; a
swelling Unnamed census is DECAY-ONSET of expressibility. Change detection
is template subtraction, performed lawfully, receipted, and journaled —
never eyeballed between two JPEGs.

## §8 — The Acquisition Duty

Every harvest playbook (PB-DATA line) MUST record the adannu-stamp at S0:
UTC time, local season, and the declared covariates available at harvest.
Where covariates are unknown they are recorded as unknown — an honest gap,
not an omitted field. The stamp travels in the Kanīku receipt.

## §9 — Proof Obligations (deposited open)

- **TPL-PO-1** — Lineage integrity: prove no operation can overwrite or
  orphan an ancestor template; succession only, NUZI-recorded.
- **TPL-PO-2** — Root persistence: across a three-epoch lineage, prove
  bit-identical roots; prove a new root can enter only via the EnkiQDB
  petition path with recorded parentage.
- **TPL-PO-3** — Cross-adannu round-trip: a particle's biography queried
  ACROSS templates reproduces exactly the per-epoch sealed values.
- **TPL-PO-4** — Fallacy demonstration: construct the industry's confusion
  (one spot, two acquisitions, "classification error") and show it resolve
  completely under adannu-stamps — the error was never error; it was time.

## §10 — Closing

The scribes did not carve one tablet for a field and call it eternal; they
recorded the field at sowing, at flood, at harvest, each tablet dated by
eponym and season, and the archive of dates WAS the knowledge. This law
returns that wisdom to the spectral age: the ground is a living citizen,
its faces are many, its roots are one, and its chronicle — not any single
portrait — is the truth of it.

*The roots never move; the world does.*
