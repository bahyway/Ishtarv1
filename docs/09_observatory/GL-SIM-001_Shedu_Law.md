## Preamble

Until this tablet, the corpus judged what exists: the aging pipe, the printed
lattice, the standing bridge. This law extends judgment to what does not yet
exist — the transformer tower before its foundation, the antenna before it
touches the roof, any new facility petitioning to join an urban Tribe. The
candidate is simulated not on a blank map but on a **witnessed membrane**:
ground the EGMS harvest already reads, mains Bārûtu already prophesies,
neighborhoods whose arrangement law Ṭēmu already knows. Installation is a
perturbation of a neighborhood's law; the Šēdu Court prophesies the cascade
before the first bolt.

## §1 — Name and Standing

This law is named **Šēdu**. It governs simulation, siting judgment, and
permitting of candidate facilities within the Ecosystem, and the passage of
any candidate from rehearsal to production. It binds every BahyWay siting
act; a facility admitted without the Šēdu rite is an unlawful citizen.

## §2 — The Rehearsal Citizen

A candidate facility is minted as a **rehearsal citizen**: a full Named
Lattice under GL-LAT-001 — every member a KAKI with tribe, class, and
erection stage — whose Temporal dimension holds **future epochs** and whose
records live in the Šala register, banner-marked, physically separate from
production ledgers. A rehearsal citizen has every dimension of a real one
and none of a real one's standing: it may be judged, compared, refused, and
destroyed without ceremony.

## §3 — The SIMULATED Register (epistemic honesty)

The GL-NIM-001 ladder is extended downward by one rung:

> MEASURED > DERIVED > ESTIMATED > ADVISED > **SIMULATED**

Every quantity produced by a Šēdu rehearsal is classed SIMULATED: honest
about being a model of a world, not a witness of one. A SIMULATED value may
inform a verdict of the Court; it may never masquerade upward, and every
Šēdu report carries the register of each figure it cites.

## §4 — The Witnessed Site Decree

A candidate is never judged against assumptions where witnesses exist. The
Court MUST ingest, for the site and its declared neighborhood radius:

- the ground witness (EGMS/PSI subsidence history at the hepta-addresses);
- the Bārûtu state of every read organ in reach (mains, structures) and the
  covariate changes the installation would cause;
- the Ḫarrānu record of buried lines crossing the works;
- the Ṭēmu arrangement law of the neighborhood Tribe;
- the census of protected particles: dwellings, schools, clinics, refuges.

Where a witness class is absent, its absence is declared in the report — an
unwitnessed dimension is named, never silently assumed benign.

## §5 — Risk as Prophesied Cascade

The candidate's risk is defined as **its new Belonging edges and the cascade
they carry** (the Kulla Cascade Clause generalized to neighborhoods):

- reach contours (fire, oil spill, blast, collapse) computed against the
  Refuge Directive for every protected particle individually;
- perturbation of read organs: changed loads, vibration, stray current,
  excavation — expressed as **shifts of their prophesied SHELL-CROSS
  epochs** (Bārûtu re-run under the counterfactual);
- ground response: the new load judged against the witnessed subsidence
  trend at the pad;
- field exposure (antennas, transformers): computed against the declared
  regulatory covenant Θ (the applicable limit standard, cited by name and
  version in the report), with margin stated. Concerns beyond what
  measurement can decide are **WITHHELD with the covenant cited** — neither
  dismissed nor invented.

## §6 — The Verdict Schema (per particle, never averaged)

The Court verdicts each candidate site:

- **REFUSED** — any single protected particle falls inside a declared reach
  contour, or any read organ's prophesied crossing moves inside its horizon,
  or the ground witness contradicts the foundation covenant. One
  kindergarten inside the oil-reach refuses the site; the Never-Averaged
  axiom admits no compensation across particles.
- **CONDITIONED** — admissible only under named conditions: monitoring
  cadence, protective works, seasonal windows; the conditions are part of
  the verdict, not advice.
- **ADMITTED** — all per-particle tests clear at declared floors.
- **WITHHELD** — the witnesses cannot carry the judgment (missing classes,
  floors unmet); more witnessing is the only lawful path forward.

Variant sites are compared **by their cascades side by side**, never by a
composite score.

## §7 — The Permit as Prescription

An ADMITTED or CONDITIONED verdict issues as an immutable **prescription
KAKI with its PROVE certificate**: install at address X under conditions Y
with monitoring cadence Z — evidence chain, registers, and floors sealed
inside, per the Analysis-to-Solution Law. The permit is a particle, not a
paper.

## §8 — The Threshold Gate (counterfactual to actual)

The passage from candidate to installed is a formal act: the Šala particle
**dies**, and the production particle is **born**, inheriting the entire
simulation history as its **pre-birth nasab** — a tower standing in 2030
forever carries what was prophesied of it in 2026. No rehearsal record ever
migrates by copy; only by this birth. Contamination of production ledgers by
SIMULATED records outside a pre-birth nasab is a corruption event.

## §9 — The Falsifiability Ledger

Every Šēdu prophecy is checkable and MUST be checked: the same instruments
that judged the simulation witness the reality on the prescribed cadence
(EGMS epochs, Bārûtu surveys, Ṭēmu statistics). Prophecy versus witness is
recorded per prediction in the **Šēdu Ledger**; the Court's accumulated
accuracy is public within the corpus. A siting court that cannot show its
record is a brochure; this one keeps score.

## §10 — Proof Obligations (deposited open)

- **SIM-PO-1** — Register integrity: prove no SIMULATED value can reach a
  production ledger except inside a pre-birth nasab (adversarial test).
- **SIM-PO-2** — Cascade fidelity: on a synthetic neighborhood with known
  physics, prophesied SHELL-CROSS shifts under installation match the
  modeled ground truth within declared bounds.
- **SIM-PO-3** — Per-particle refusal: demonstrate a variant "acceptable on
  average" is REFUSED when one protected particle breaches its contour.
- **SIM-PO-4** — Falsifiability round-trip: a rehearsal prophecy, a gate
  passage, and a later witness epoch reconcile in the Šēdu Ledger with the
  prophecy scored.

## §11 — Closing

The old builders set the šēdu at the gate before anyone slept inside: the
guardian preceded the habitation. This tablet restores the order of
operations to modern building — judgment before installation, prophecy
before permission, and a score kept forever on the prophets.
