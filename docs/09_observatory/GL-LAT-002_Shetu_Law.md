## Preamble

A point cloud is a crowd; a city is a crowd with addresses. The scan-lines
that decorate the 4D projects' clouds are accidents of the sensor's sweep;
this tablet seals the same weave as LAW: every civil surface receives a
deterministic, quantized, addressable lattice — the šētu — so that a
facade in Najaf, a narrow alley, a buried main, and a photovoltaic array
are no longer four problems but ONE: cells on a net, walked, judged, and
storied by the same algebra. This is the master key of the civil BigRing.

## §1 — Name and Standing

The law is **Šētu**. It governs the casting, addressing, stitching, and
walking of surface lattices on every civil structure in the Ecosystem, and
it is the SOLE lawful addressing scheme for civil-service particles. A
service that invents its own coordinates is an unlawful cartographer.

## §2 — The Chart Doctrine (how a surface receives its net)

Every civil surface is assigned, at enrollment, a **chart** from the sealed
chart classes:

- **PLANE** — facades, walls, panel tables;
- **RIBBON** — roads, alleys, canals: a centerline with width, u along,
  v across;
- **CYLINDER** — pipes, columns, tanks: u along axis, v around girth;
- **ARRAY** — regular manufactured grids (solar farms): u,v by row and
  column of the physical unit.

The chart, its origin, its orientation, and its seed are sealed per
structure in its Kanīku. Charts are deterministic: the same structure
enrolled twice receives the identical net, forever.

## §3 — Pāṭu Quantization and the Fixed-Address Theorem

Each service declares its **cell size** per chart class (navigation ribbon
0.5 m; pipe cylinder 1.0 m along-axis; solar ARRAY = one physical cell;
facade 0.5 m) — declared before casting, per Pāṭu. Every cell is minted a
KAKI citizen whose address is the triple:

> **(structure_id, u, v)** — plus the d* shell where depth applies.

> **The Fixed-Address Theorem:** the per-cell schema is identical across
> all chart classes and all services — address, KAKI, register, verdict,
> harmony coordinates where spectra exist. A facade cell, an alley cell, a
> pipe metre, and a PV cell are the SAME KIND OF CITIZEN. New services
> lengthen the overlays — never the schema.

## §4 — The Seam Clause (stitching without gaps or double claims)

Where charts meet — facade to street, street to street, pipe rising to a
valve — the seam is governed: **border witnesses** are minted on both
sides (per GL-MAP-001's gapless guarantee), every seam cell names its
partner across the seam, and no point of the city is claimed twice or
claimed never. An unstitched seam is an open wound in the net and blocks
approval of the enrollment.

## §5 — Ḫarrānu on the Net (navigation as lattice walk)

A route is a **facet walk**: adjacent cells on the lattice, seams crossed
only at stitched pairs. Therefore:

- the Najaf Navigation Engine reduces to walking RIBBON cells —
  and **narrowness becomes arithmetic**: an alley's passability for a
  cart, an ambulance, a funeral procession is its ribbon's width IN CELLS,
  a verdict (PASSABLE-ALL / PASSABLE-FOOT / IMPASSABLE-VEHICLE) minted per
  segment, not guessed per trip;
- the water grid is walked on CYLINDER cells beneath the ribbons (d*
  shells), so "which valve isolates this leak" is a lattice cut, and the
  Palgu single-dig inherits addresses natively;
- the solar farm is walked on ARRAY cells, where each PV unit carries its
  own SafaaHarmony: a hot-spot is a **shed cell** on the net, a failing
  string is a **run of disharmony along u** — detection is reading the
  weave.

## §6 — Service Overlays (one net, many garments)

Services attach to cells as overlays — navigation costs, hydraulic
membership, electrical string membership, ownership, Puluḫtu — never as
rival coordinate systems. Cross-service questions become single sentences:

```
WITNESS cells WHERE structure=alley_74 AND overlay.water.leak=CONVICTED
ORBIT BY u        -- where along THIS alley is the dig?

WITNESS cells WHERE chart=ARRAY AND shed_deg > floor
ORBIT BY structure, v   -- which strings, on which tables, are sickening?
```

## §7 — The Master-Key Clause (the civil BigRing)

Every enrolled structure is a **ring**: its cells are the ring's particles,
ordered by (u,v); the city's structures assemble as the **Civil BigRing**
— facades, alleys, mains, and arrays as concentric orbits around the
municipal nucleus, with the quarantine ring receiving the unstitched, the
contradicted, and the unnameable. State is position on the net; change is
motion along it; and every court of this corpus — Zīmu, Adannu, Askuppu,
Palgu, Puluḫtu — now speaks about the same addressed citizens. The net is
the master key because it makes the city ONE data structure.
