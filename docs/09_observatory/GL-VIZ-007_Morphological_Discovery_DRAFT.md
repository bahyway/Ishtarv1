## 0 · The Claim

BahyWay claims a distinct innovation in data structuring: **Morphological
Discovery** — rendering a data corpus as a *living form* whose every visual
property is a faithful reading of a real, sealed quantity, so that latent
patterns (hidden dependencies, fraying links, decaying regions, true concept
attraction) become perceptible in the shape itself — patterns a table or a
static ERD cannot surface. The corpus reveals its own vital essence.

This is not "biology-styled dataviz." The distinction, and the whole basis of
the claim, is that the metaphor is **semantically load-bearing** (§1): nothing
is decorative; every motion means a number.

## 2 · The Honesty Coupling (what makes it science, not spectacle)

A morphology can mislead if the viewer mistakes art for finding. Two clauses
bind the claim:

- **D-1 · Morphology proposes; algebra proves.** A pattern seen in the shape is
  a *candidate*, never a conclusion. It becomes a finding only when confirmed by
  a HeptaScript query against the same particles (ORBIT/PROVE at the relevant
  SCOPE). The eye discovers; the theorem certifies.
- **D-2 · Every visual cites its number.** Any rendered property must be
  traceable to the sealed EAV/topology value that produced it (hover-readable in
  the prototype; provenance-linked in production). No unbacked motion exists.

Together these make Morphological Discovery a **hypothesis engine** feeding the
Unified Algebra — not a replacement for it. Discovery-by-morphology widens what
a human notices; the algebra decides what is true.
