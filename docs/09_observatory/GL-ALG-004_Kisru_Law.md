## §1 — Name and Standing

A **kiṣru** is a named task-force of arsenal members bound to deliver ONE
Ecosystem solution. Each kiṣru declares: its **mission**, its **muster**
(members by name), its **witness chain** (which independent channels
convict, per Napharu §5), its **deliverable**, and its **scoring ledger**.
Membership is many-to-many: a calculus may serve several kiṣru, as
officers serve several campaigns. The per-member tier census remains in
Borsippa; this tablet binds the formations.

## §3 — The Joint-Operations Clause

Kiṣru share members and MUST share witnesses lawfully: when two kiṣru
convict at the same hepta-address, their witness channels must remain
independent (Napharu §5) — a shared sensor may serve both estimates but
counts ONCE toward any conviction. K8 binds all: no kiṣru may relax a
Meta law for its mission's convenience.

## §4 — The Liveness Clause (keeping the Arsenal alive)

Every kiṣru keeps a **muster ledger** (REGISTRY-KISRU + LEDGER-MUSTER):
mission, members, open Proof Obligations, and **last drill** — the UTC
date its POs or gates last ran green. A kiṣru undrilled beyond its
declared interval is flagged **DORMANT** in the Arsenal Atlas's Šurinnu
stele — hollow cell, named absence — until a drill restores it. The
Arsenal stays live not by oath but by scheduled proof.

## §5 — The Accession Clause

Every future calculus (member 56 onward) declares, at enrollment, which
kiṣru it joins and which witnesses it brings — a member without a
formation is inventory, and inventory is refused.
