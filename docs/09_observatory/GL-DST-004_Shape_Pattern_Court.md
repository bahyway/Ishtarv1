## 1. Placement Decree — why PDM, and only PDM

The court is a **tab in DubSar PDM**, not a second IDE, and not a Theater or Girsu tab, because governance must sit where the custody verbs live. DubSar Theater holds the verbs *prove, explore, derive* — it has no approval authority. Girsu holds *write, compile* — code, not custody. **DubSar PDM holds *correct, approve, release*** — and a veto is real only where it can disable the very button it governs. Mathematics computed elsewhere (Lamassu, OntoGraph, Nasaru) is witnessed here; the surface judges, engines compute (GL-PHY-001 discipline).

## 2. The Bench — three instruments, one bridge

- **I · Shape Reader (Lamassu-backed):** persistence over the tribe's Hepta positions — Betti curves b₀, b₁, …, Euler characteristic, barcode, census N. Drawn flat; the chart never bends.
- **II · Lattice (OntoGraph):** the concept order with ⊤, reading Mandatory EAV, writing harvest only.
- **III · Mint (Nebuchadnezzar):** GL-TPL-001 concord — a Unified Pattern strikes **only where shape witness and lattice witness agree**: `onto.betti_signature ⨝ onto.concept_id`. Two mathematics, one verdict.

**Minting Stability Gate:** no Nebuchadnezzar mint while MARKASU or RIGMU is active on the tribe. Patterns are minted from homeostasis, never from turbulence — a drifting shape is not yet a shape.

## 3. The Two Named Harms of a Correction

A steward correction to Data Shapes is lawful work — but it can wound. The court names the wounds:

- **Curve Loss (topology harm):** the correction erases persistent features — a Betti curve dies, a loop closes, a component merges — measured as `betti_diff(before, after)` against the shape's sealed baseline (the **Temennu-of-Shape**: the enrolled betti_signature, re-laid only by decree, per GL-NSR-001-A1 §1 discipline).
- **Census Loss (totality harm):** particles overlooked — dropped, orphaned, or silently excluded — measured as `census_diff`, a direct breach test of the **Point-in-Time Totality** theorem.

## 4. The Nabalkutu Register (the juggling particles)

Particles whose state or position flaps — oscillating between corrections, failing the OU stability gate, flipping GOLDEN↔FUZZY — are registered as **nabalkutu** (*to leap over, to rebel, to overturn*; the name is unspent). The nabalkutu census is what keeps Markasu ringing on a shape. A shape with a non-empty nabalkutu register is *sub judice*: still before the court.

## 5. THE BELL VETO (the law of this tablet)

> **While MARKASU or RIGMU is active on a tribe — while its nabalkutu register is non-empty — approval of its Data Shape (SC, Simplicial Complexity class) and release into version KAKIv4.0 are PROHIBITED, regardless of Data Steward signature.**

- The steward's signature is a **witness, never a verdict**. Approval requires the co-witnesses: bells quiet, census total, curves accounted.
- The approve and release controls are **physically disabled** in the PDM surface while the veto holds — not warned, disabled. A warning can be clicked through; a veto cannot.
- **Loss-Explanation Clause:** a correction that causes Curve Loss or Census Loss is not automatically unlawful — lawful losses exist (true-duplicate removal via ShalaEngine, decreed retirements). But every loss must carry an **explanation particle** (W5H2-shaped, per GL-NSR-001-A2) *before* approval. Unexplained loss = veto, even with silent bells.
- **Override path — never silent:** the veto yields only to a **Madanu decree bearing the Architect's seal (CSR-08)**, journaled in NĀRU with the decree text, the dissenting bell state, and the τ cost. There is no administrative override, no steward escalation, no quiet path. The bell outranks the seal; only the court outranks the bell.
