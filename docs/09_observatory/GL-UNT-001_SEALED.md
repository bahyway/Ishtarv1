## §0 Standing units (unchanged)
`PU` — the tribe's whitened orbit-radius σ.
`OU` — healthy girth G₀ (Nanshe/HSI).
Nothing in this tablet redefines either.

## §1 Radius Unit — RU
`RU` is the peak-to-peak spacing of a tribe's **radial density** measured from its heart.
A citizen's radius in RU carries an integer part (its **layer**) and a fractional part (its seat inside the layer).
RU is tribe-relative and dimensionless: **3.2 RU means the same thing in every tribe**, in a water tribe and a burial-ground tribe alike.
Rendering law: integer RU draws as a **contour line** around the heart. Never as a flat grid.

## §2 Membrane Layer Unit — MLU
`MLU` is **one factor-leaf crossing**.
A citizen's MLU relative to a reference leaf is the count of membranes between them.
An OntoGraph edge's MLU is its **piercing count**.
RU is radial, MLU is transverse. Together they give every citizen and every edge a two-number address:
how central it is, and how deep it sits.
Rendering law: leaves draw as translucent **shells** above and below the membrane, never as flat bands.

## §3 Disclosure boundary
SUSA exposes exactly `RU ≥ k ∧ |MLU| ≤ m`.
The heart and the deep crossings never leave.
A federation of BIGRINGs publishes the **union of exposed shells**, each measured in its own RU.
`k` and `m` are set per BIGRING by its owner, and are recorded as a Kanīku receipt at the moment of sharing.

## §4 Deep leak
An edge with `MLU ≥ 2` is a **deep leak**.
It is WITNESSed across two epochs before any verdict fires (GL-AGE-001 two-witness rule).
A single epoch yields **suspicion**, never verdict.

## §5 Radial-active factor (admits GL-ALG-003)
A factor is **radial-active** in a tribe when its leaf mixture differs between rings beyond a two-epoch-witnessed threshold; it is **inert** otherwise.
The ranking of factors by radial activity is the court's answer to *why this crowd is here*.
The Fadam functional adjudicates which foliation makes the crowd's edges most ordered; that foliation names the cause.

## §6 Mixed layers
Leaves are **ecosystem-wide, not tribe-wide**: the same leaf runs along the whole BIGRING and every tribe's citizens orbit it.
Neighbouring tribes' RU contours may overlap.
A citizen seated inside a neighbour's contour is a **guest**: it keeps its birth tribe in `κ[4..5]` and records its seat in EAV. The KAKI byte layout is not touched.
A thread joining citizens of two tribes is a **cross-tribe leak** and is WITNESSed by both tribes' courts.

## §7 Amendment
Amendments are numbered `GL-UNT-001-A1`, `-A2`, … and require a fresh CSR-08 rite.
No amendment may alter §3; the disclosure boundary is the sovereignty clause of this tablet.
