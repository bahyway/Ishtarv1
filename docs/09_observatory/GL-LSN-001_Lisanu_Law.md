## PREAMBLE — The Wound and the Graft

Notation trails discovery. When the same deep pattern recurs across engines and
must be glossed three different ways, the corpus is wounded: the tongue cannot
speak what the algebra already knows. This law charters an agent that detects
such wounds and proposes grafts — dialect candidates cut from the body's own
flesh, the Unified Algebra itself — while reserving the act of sealing to the
Architect alone.

*That struggle is the wound; the dialect is the graft.*

---

## §1 — THE WOUND TRIGGER (Three-Gloss, Two-Witness)

1.1. A **structural signature** is the canonical shape of a pattern family,
derived from GL-ALG-002 face decomposition (Particle / Orbit / Tribe).

1.2. A **wound** exists when one structural signature has accumulated **three
or more distinct Ḫubullu glosses** (GL-NAM-002 plain-language glosses) across
the corpus. Three glosses for one shape is proof the shared notation is missing.

1.3. A wound may only *trigger* graft-minting when witnessed by **two or more
independent engines** (the Two-Witness rule, sibling to GL-AGE-001 Šību).
A wound seen by one engine alone remains OBSERVED, never TRIGGERED.

## §2 — WHAT THE AGENT MAY PROPOSE (Uruk-Only)

2.1. The agent MAY mint **unsealed dialect tablets**: grammar-candidate
productions derived exclusively from the triggered wound's structural
signature and the sealed arsenal of GL-ALG-002.

2.2. All proposals are born in the **URUK stream** (GL-OPS-002) as files under
`uruk/`, marked `UNSEALED`. The agent possesses **no write path to Kish**.
No function, task, or code path of the agent shall move, copy, or promote a
tablet into `kish/`.

2.3. The agent MAY extract and refine **template molds** (Nalbanu-style,
deterministic, never ML) from already-sealed patterns.

2.4. The agent MAY recommend — in journal prose only — that a dialect family
has matured enough to deserve the Architect's attention.

## §3 — WHAT ONLY BAHAA MAY SEAL

3.1. The **Kish crossing**: promotion of any dialect tablet from `uruk/` to
`kish/` occurs only through a numbered playbook run by Bahaa (CSR-08 —
running the playbook is the confirmation act).

3.2. **New axioms.** The agent extends within the algebra's closure; it is
structurally blind to what the axioms make invisible. Any change to the axiom
base of GL-ALG-002 requires the external witness — Bahaa's word.

3.3. **AkkadianAOL core grammar.** Dialects live *under* AkkadianAOL as
sub-grammars the code generator learns to emit — flesh of the same body,
never a second person. The core language is untouchable by the agent.

3.4. **Names.** Per NL-001, engine and dialect naming is sealed by Bahaa alone.

## §4 — FOREIGN-TISSUE REJECTION (The Immune Clause)

4.1. Any candidate production containing non-sovereign constructs is
**REJECTED as foreign tissue** before staging. Non-exhaustive blocklist:
SQL keywords (SELECT, FROM, WHERE, JOIN, EXISTS, INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE),
embedded foreign DSL directives, or any designation of AkkadianAOL or its
dialects as a "DSL".

4.2. Rejection is recorded with cause in the NĀRU journal. No silent
rejection, no silent close (Simmu discipline, GL-HSI-001-A3/A4).

## §5 — THE AUTOGRAFT PRINCIPLE

5.1. Every graft carries the genome: the triality **τ + ε ≥ ε(Θ)**, the three
faces (Particle / Orbit / Tribe), and W5H2 predicate compatibility. A candidate
failing the genome check is not a graft but a tumor, and is refused.

5.2. Regulatory layer against self-similar overgrowth: no more than **one
dialect candidate per wound**, and no candidate may cite another unsealed
candidate as ancestry. Grafts grow from sealed flesh only.

## §6 — ADJUDICATION

6.1. Contested cases (conflicting witnesses, ambiguous genome check, gloss
disputes) are routed to the **Fadam functional** for adjudication.

6.2. Verdict vocabulary: `OBSERVED` → `TRIGGERED` → `MINTED` →
`REJECTED-FOREIGN` / `REJECTED-TUMOR` / `STAGED-URUK` → (Bahaa only)
`SEALED-KISH`.

## §7 — THE JOURNAL (No Silent Close)

7.1. Every wound receives a verdict line in the append-only **NĀRU journal**
(JSON Lines). Every verdict names its witnesses, its signature, and its cause.

7.2. Z3 does not appear in this agent. It is runtime-free of solvers
(design-time verification remains MUMMU/Gate G4 territory).

## ḪUBULLU GLOSS (GL-NAM-002 — plain language, mandatory)

> **In plain words:** This law creates a scribe-assistant that watches for
> moments when the same idea keeps needing different explanations in different
> parts of the system. When that happens in at least two places, it drafts a
> small shared notation for that idea — built only from rules we already
> agreed on — and puts the draft in the experimental pile. It can never make
> anything official, never touch the core language, never invent new
> foundations, and never use borrowed database syntax. Only Bahaa promotes a
> draft to law, by running a playbook.
