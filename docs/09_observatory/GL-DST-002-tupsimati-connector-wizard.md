## Clause 1 — Fourteen Doors, One Rite
The wizard presents all fourteen EnkiDB nodes as bindable
destinies: seven WRITE nodes (EnkiSDB..EnkiDDB, ports
7001-7007) and seven READ nodes (ports 7101-7107), as
deployed in Podman quadlets. Binding is a ConEngine rite:
HEPT handshake (magic 0x48455054), session sealed as an
immutable KAKI Connection Event, provenance on every
byte that crosses. READ is the steward's default; WRITE
binding requires explicit Architect selection.

## Clause 2 — Every House Owns Its Own BIGRING
Upon binding, the ORBIT 3D lens renders the bound house's
OWN BIGRING: only the tribe orbits whose particles are
camped in that database, re-laid as quantized concentric
shells with sacred void between them. The whole-pipeline
BIGRING (all houses superposed) is the unbound default.
Data mining is thereby lawful over EVERY station of the
Flow Particles Pipeline — never over EnkiDW alone.

## Clause 3 — The Census Precedes the Query
The bound view carries live counters: particles per orbit
(colour and state) per house, average dwell, and the
count of particles AWAITING STEWARD DECISION (FUZZY past
the dwell threshold). Counters are computed by each Enki
node from its own ENLIL ring indexes and returned over
HEPT — the Theater witnesses totals, it never counts.
Every counter is a query launcher: selecting it composes
the corresponding HeptaScript (WHERE home, WHAT state,
WHEN dwell > Nd) for the Architect to run.

## Clause 4 — The Chronicle Is a Must
Picking any particle in any bound house retrieves its
complete pipeline event history from StoryEngine over
NUZI: genesis, every orbit shift with its KISPU commit,
dwell at every station, PROVE certificate or death rite.
The chronicle is the etiology behind the census's
symptom — it is how the steward answers WHY a particle
has existed so long in a station.

## Clause 5 — Stage, Never Truth; Advise, Never Decide
The wizard reads and witnesses. Steward judgments made at
the stage EMIT advisory Recommendation particles
(NINSUN/Namtila pattern — never blocking, never
mutating). State transitions execute exclusively in the
sovereign Rust engines behind HEPT, upon the Architect's
CSR-08 confirmation. Any Godot-side write to particle
state is a violation of this law and of GL-DST-001.
