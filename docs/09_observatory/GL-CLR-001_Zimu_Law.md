## Preamble

An RGB pixel is three numbers; a multispectral pixel is a handful of
disconnected bars; only a hyperspectral pixel is a **continuous curve** — and
continuity is what makes deviation *measurable to a degree* rather than
binned into steps. This tablet seals the consequence: in BahyWay, the colour
of a particle is never a free value. It is a **root** (the canonical
signature of its Tribe) and a **shed degree** (how far this particle's curve
has drifted from that root), riding on a **luminance surface**. The mechanism
is named SafaaHarmony, for purity and clarity are exactly what it measures.

## §1 — Name and Standing

The law is **Zīmu**; the mechanism is **SafaaHarmony**. It governs colour
representation, harmony auditing, and template approval for every
hyperspectral (and, degraded honestly, multispectral) image entering the
Ecosystem, and it is chartered as **the official BahyWay ETL State Coloring
Harmony Mechanism across stations S0–S8**. No spectral image becomes a
sealed template except through this law's Transit Rite (§8).

## §2 — The Decomposition (surface × mask)

Every particle's spectrum splits lawfully into:

- **the luminance surface** — the magnitude ‖X‖: brightness, illumination ×
  albedo; the black-and-white body of the image, one scalar per cell;
- **the colour mask** — the direction X/‖X‖: the spectral *shape*, the part
  the molds are cast from.

> particle = luminance × ( Σₖ abundanceₖ · moldₖ )

True RGB is neither of these: it is a **derived rendering**, computed on
demand from the mixture through the observer's response curves, CLASS
DERIVED like every regeneration.

## §3 — ColourID as Root-of-Tribe

The ColourID of a particle names its **root**: the mold whose canonical
signature governs it. Roots are Nalbanu castings, archived in Borsippa,
each sealed with its Kanīku. A particle does not *have* a colour; it
*descends from* one.

## §4 — The Shed Degree (continuous deviation)

The **shed degree** is the measured deviation of the particle's mask from
its root: the spectral angle to the mold (degrees), with an optional **shed
direction** (toward which neighbouring root the drift leans). Shed is a
continuous quantity, journaled per cell, and its trajectory over epochs is
first-class Nanshe material: a Tribe whose shed distribution drifts fires
INFLECTION; a cell crossing the declared shed ceiling approaches the
Unnamed (§7).

## §5 — The Harmony-by-Continuity Clause

Harmony is not an aesthetic; it is **earned by the continuity of the
spectrum**. Only hyperspectral measurement makes distance-between-colours a
smooth, well-defined angle and shed a graded quantity. RGB admits metamers
— chemically different materials wearing identical triples — which is false
harmony and inadmissible as evidence of Tribe. Multispectral yields coarse
shed and is admitted only with its coarseness declared. Where linear mixing
bends (intimate mixtures, moisture), the bending is journaled as lawful
INFLECTION, never hidden.

## §6 — The Fixed-Schema Theorem (band-count independence)

> **The per-particle EAV schema of a spectral image is fixed and
> independent of band count.**

A 200-band cube, a 242-band cube, and tomorrow's 400-band sensor all store
the same per-particle attributes: root ColourID, shed degree (and optional
direction), luminance, residual, and Kanīku. The bands live **once**, in
the mold registry; particles carry only their harmony coordinates. New
instruments lengthen the molds — **never the schema**. EnkiDB does not
bloat because the spectrum belongs to the Tribe, not to the citizen.

## §7 — The Unnamed-Residual Guardian

The residual is sacred. A cell whose mask the harmony cannot express —
residual above the declared floor, no root within the declared shed
ceiling — is never forced into a shed degree. It stands **Unnamed**, its
raw deviation quarantined toward EnkiQDB as a candidate new mold. A
harmony that silently rounds away the inexpressible is a lie wearing a
beautiful gown; SafaaHarmony refuses the gown.

## §8 — The Transit Rite (harmony audited before the template is fixed)

No image becomes a sealed template by computation alone. The Court
**transits the membrane Tribe by Tribe**, and for each Tribe audits, at
declared floors set before the transit:

- the **mean shed** and **worst shed** of its members;
- the **residual census** (Unnamed candidates inside its hull);
- the **root integrity** (the mold's Kanīku re-verified).

Per-Tribe verdicts: **HARMONIOUS** (within floors), **CONDITIONED**
(admitted with a named remediation — e.g., split the root, re-cast the
mold), or **UNNAMED-RICH** (too much inexpressible: the template is not yet
worthy). Only when every Tribe stands HARMONIOUS or lawfully CONDITIONED is
the template **FIXED AND APPROVED**: its ontograph is emitted, its EAV
schema instantiated (per §6, always the same shape), and the whole sealed
with Kanīku into the corpus. Until that moment it is a candidate, banner-
marked, with no standing.

## §9 — The KAKI Clause (why SafaaHarmony requires KAKI v4.0)

The mechanism stands on immutability, and this clause records it
explicitly, at the architect's word:

1. **Roots never move.** A mold, once sealed, is immutable — therefore a
   shed degree measured today and one measured in ten years are measured
   against the *same* root, and harmony is auditable across decades. A
   mutable baseline would make every shed degree a moving lie.
2. **The colour triple is closed.** Per EN-MDB-001, colour is stored as the
   (D6.r, D6.g, D6.b) triple *because KAKI v4.0 is immutable* — the display
   rendering of the root is fixed at sealing; SafaaHarmony inherits this.
3. **Shed history is append-only.** A cell's shed trajectory accumulates as
   immutable epochs; the harmony's INFLECTIONs are permanent record.
4. **Supersession, never mutation.** When a root must change (a Tribe
   splits, a mold is re-cast), a new KAKI is born and the lineage is
   recorded in NUZI; the old root remains, and old shed degrees remain
   true against it.

Without KAKI v4.0, SafaaHarmony would be an opinion; with it, it is a
measurement with a chain of custody.
