## 1. Purpose — judgment requires a standard

The court (GL-DST-004) can veto a wounded shape but cannot yet *name* what the shape has become. There is no external standard for "simplicial standard shapes of data" — so this tablet seals BahyWay's own: a closed registry of named forms, each with a BWVL glyph, its witnesses, its Triple-O meaning, and its court verdict. Every tribe classifies to **exactly one** Ṣalmu (totality of classification, L20).

## 2. The Apsu Vacancy Principle

In Orbit-Oriented Ontology, particles live in orbit around a center that is **empty by law** — the Apsu belongs to the law, not to particles. Therefore *center occupation is a finding, not a style*: a filled interior means particles exist where no orbital observation lives, and the first suspect is **fabricated interior** — imputation, interpolation, or aggregation presented as observed fact. This principle is what the KIRṢU verdict enforces.

## 3. The Three Witness Classes (Betti alone is not enough)

b₀ = b₁ = identical for a solid disk, a filament, and a crescent. Classification therefore uses:

- **W-A · Betti at standard radius ε:** (b₀, b₁) from the union-of-balls witness.
- **W-B · The Barû Sweep (persistence):** b₁ across a radius filtration r ∈ [r_min, r_max]. A hole's *lifetime* distinguishes a true orbit (long-lived) from a **ghost orbit** (a hole absent at ε that appears under the sweep — the crescent's near-loop).
- **W-C · Mass geometry:** core occupancy κ (mass fraction within 0.45·R_gyration of the centroid) and elongation e = λ₁/λ₂ of the covariance ellipse.

- **History-aware classification:** ŠIBIRTU vs EPERU is decided by the Temennu-of-Shape ancestry, never by geometry alone — the same dust means differently if it was once a hoop.
