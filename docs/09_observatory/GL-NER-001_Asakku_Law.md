## §1 · SECTOR SIGHT (ḫiāṭu — the watch)
The court SHALL identify, per storage sector and per VM storage unit:
  (a) WHICH sector is under attack (threat tribe attribution), and
  (b) the EXTENT of compromised space within that sector, rendered as a
      field (fraction of the sector's capacity), never as an enumeration.
Extent is a first-class witnessed quantity: extent_q ∈ [0,1], log-legible,
shown on the membrane as arc coverage and in the census as a number.

## §2 · FRAGMENT INQUEST (the wound and the copy)
For data judged TOTALLY CORRUPTED (irrecoverable in place), the court SHALL:
  (a) pinpoint the fragment (identity, VM, sector, tribe, seal), and
  (b) render the BACKUP VERDICT from the NUZI lineage:
        BACKUP-HELD  — a NUZI archive copy exists; restoration is a rite.
        UNBACKED     — no lineage; the only copy IS the wound. ALARM class.
No fragment verdict may be withheld from the steward. The inquest list is
an EXHIBIT organ (top-K), not a census — the census remains mass.

## §3 · THE ASAKKU THRESHOLD (the halt of the workshops)
Let ρ(vm) = infected-data density within a VM storage unit.
  IF ρ(vm) > 0.30 for ANY vm  →  ASAKKU HALT:
    (a) ALERT — Nisaba-class alert event, Kittu delivery, journal entry;
    (b) SEAL ALL WORKING ENVIRONMENTS — DubSar Theater IDE, DubSar PDM IDE,
        Girsu IDE — state RUNNING → SEALED·ASAKKU. No new work enters a
        sealed workshop; in-flight work is checkpointed, never destroyed.
    (c) The halt is LIFTED only by the steward's act (CSR-08 discipline),
        and the LIFT is REFUSED while ρ remains above threshold — the
        steward may not overrule the plague, only confirm its passing.
The threshold 0.30 is a declared covenant of this law (RU-Ledger style):
it may be amended by tablet, never adjusted silently.

## §4 · DISCLOSURE CLAUSE (the anti-secrecy oath)
Everything in §1–§3 SHALL be visible to every stakeholder on the standing
dashboards (Agû / Nergal Membrane): the sector, the extent, the fragments,
the backup verdicts, the density gauges, the halt state, and the lift acts.
The court does not possess a private ledger. What Nergal knows, the
stakeholder sees. This clause is the doctrine's edge over commercial AV,
which lacks these organs or fails to disclose them.
