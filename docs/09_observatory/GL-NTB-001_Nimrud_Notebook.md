## 0. Normative Orientation

The Notebook is the stakeholder's console over the whole arc: HeptaScript cells whose
results render as the four views — **Orbit, Data, Membrane, Particles** — all joined by
one key, the hepta-address. It is a **stage, never truth** (GL-DST-001): it reads the
HeptaSpaceMap index, OntoGraph (written by Enūma Eliš, read by Nimrud), and the sealed
verdicts; it writes nothing past its own journal. GeoEngine remains the single
mathematical truth source; EnkiDB remains the record.

## 1. The Language Law — Clause B1

Every cell is **HeptaScript v1.1** — five sovereign operations (ORBIT / EMIT / PROVE /
SYNC / WITNESS), W5H2 clauses, WINDOW, geometric nouns. **SQL is refused at parse**,
not errored: a SELECT/FROM/JOIN cell receives a REFUSAL RECORD deposed to the journal,
the same dignity given every unborn thing (ABSOLUTE QUERY LAW restated; WHERE/WHEN
remain lawful W5H2 words).

## 2. Stage Never Truth — Clause B2

The Notebook is read-only against the record. EMIT from a notebook cell routes through
SUSA and the full gate chain like any external act — and in rehearsal, EMIT is disabled
outright. No view is authoritative: a rendered orbit, dent, or cell is theater; the
number behind it lives in EnkiDB and is fetched, never recomputed on stage.

## 3. The Address Is the Join Key — Clause B3

All four views bind on **hepta-address × epoch window**. Orbit shows the tribe's
standing over the window; Data shows the W5H2 verdict tablet; Membrane shows the strain
at the address; Particles shows the owned cell at depth d\*. One address, four
projections — the index lookup and the stakeholder's story remain the same walk
(GL-NIM-002 P4).

## 4. Stamps Travel — Clause B4

Query results inherit their epistemic classes (GL-NIM-001 N1): MEASURED / DERIVED /
ESTIMATED / ADVISED chips and ε ride into every view. **A view never strips the class** —
a chart of an estimate is still an estimate, and says so on its face.

## 5. Every Run Is a Rite — Clause B5

Execution is append-only journaled through StoryEngine: cell text, result summary,
stamps, timestamp. A notebook is a reproducible rite, not a scratchpad — replaying the
cells replays the interrogation, and the journal is its nasab chain.

## 6. Read Path

Enūma Eliš writes OntoGraph (RELATE). The Nimrud Stack reads it for verdict reasoning.
The Notebook reads **both** and writes **neither** (rehearsal), standing where the
stakeholder stands: outside the courts, with full sight and no hands.
