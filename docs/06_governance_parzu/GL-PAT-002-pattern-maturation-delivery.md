# GL-PAT-002 — Pattern Maturation & Template Delivery Law
**Status:** SEALED (concept). Implementation queued behind PB-160.
**Owners:** the OTA/staging orbits (GL-PAT-001 quarantine),
the drift & harmony instrument (GL-TPL-002, Lamassu β's),
TemplateEngine (GL-TPL-001 mint), the release process
(NL-001 eras), and the Architect alone.

## Clause 1 — Proving Across Many Lifecycles, Many Clients
A quarantined foreign pattern earns nothing from a single
passing rehearsal. It must prove itself across MULTIPLE
lifecycles and MULTIPLE clients' data — old tribes and
newly onboarded ones — inside OTA. One client helped is
luck; a dozen tribes improved is evidence. The proving
period is explicit and chronicled (StoryEngine).

## Clause 2 — Harmony as the Fitness Function
The measure of a pattern's worth is whether it strengthens
the SYMMETRIC HARMONY of the orbits, judged by the
topological instrument across the proving set:
- earlier DETECTION of defects (dwell-to-detection falls);
- cleaner ISOLATION (β₀ islands resolve rather than
  multiply);
- genuine DISCOVERY (defects the old patterns missed now
  surface);
- no HARM (β₁/β₂ do not worsen; no existing pattern is
  pulled out of true).
A pattern that improves harmony across many populations has
earned promotion; one that helps a single client, or that
degrades symmetry anywhere, has not. Fitness is Darwinian:
OTA is the selection pressure.

## Clause 3 — Promotion Is the Architect's Sole Act
Promotion depends on the accumulated multi-client evidence
and is the Architect's non-delegable judgment (GL-PAT-001
§6). No agent, no advisory layer, no automated rite may
promote. The decision and its evidence are sealed.

## Clause 4 — Metamorphosis: Pattern → Template
While proving, the insight is a PATTERN — an internal file,
arsenal-resident, KAKI-identified, the Architect's IP. On
promotion it undergoes the GL-TPL-001 minting rite and
becomes a TEMPLATE — a deliverable artifact. The Pattern is
what is tested; the Template is what is shipped. Lineage
links the Template to its Pattern and to the OTA proving
record.

## Clause 5 — Cross-Client Delivery, Abstraction Absolute
A Template proven across Client-A, -B, -C and delivered to
Client-D contains the abstracted defect-detection SHAPE and
NONE of A/B/C's records, identifiers, or reconstructable
fragments (GL-TPL-001 §4, carried fully into the foreign
path). Multi-client proving occurs in OTA on licensed
access; only the generalized shape crosses back out.
This is what makes cross-client learning a lawful feature
rather than a breach: shape flows, data never does.

## Clause 6 — Delivery Is a Versioned, Consented Release
The enriched Template reaches clients holding a prior
release ONLY as an accumulative update or a newly purchased
release — sealed (Ed25519), lineage-bearing, king-named era
(NL-001), and CONSENTED: the client sees what changed and
why, and chooses to take it. A governance product never
silently mutates its own rules; delivery is as append-only
and consented as every other truth (GL-DST-003).

## Clause 7 — The Compounding Engine
The full arc: a competitor's public pattern, observed
lawfully (Dayyālu) → minted FOREIGN → proven across the
client base in the OTA airlock → promoted by the
Architect's sole hand → metamorphosed into a Template →
delivered back to paying clients as new value. Every
engagement makes the arsenal smarter; every tool observed
makes the product better; and the value flows, abstracted
and consented, to those who purchased the covenant. The
scout walks the world; the airlock digests what it brings;
the clients are enriched — and no one's data ever leaves
their own tribe.
