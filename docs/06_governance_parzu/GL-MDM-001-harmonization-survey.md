## Clause 1 — Survey Scope
The harmonization survey runs CROSS-CUTTING: across
multiple tribes (client departments), multiple houses
(all seven EnkiDB types, WRITE and READ), and multiple
BIGRINGs (per client, per house). Its object is the
SHAPE — the Template inferred by the DataStructure
Station per department's data — never the record.
GL-TPL-001 Clause 4 abstraction governs absolutely:
shapes may be compared across tenants; values may not.

## Clause 2 — The Two-Witness Shape Verdict
Same-entity candidacy requires two independent
witnesses (the ecosystem's standing verdict pattern,
third application):
  (a) STRUCTURAL — Jaccard over shape shingles
      (normalized field paths, type pairs, nesting
      signatures), computed as MinHash signatures with
      LSH banding so the survey scales across every
      BIGRING at once;
  (b) SEMANTIC — NabuEngine embedding proximity of
      field names and shape descriptions, bridging
      languages and naming cultures (KLANT_NAAM ~
      customer_name), fully offline via candle.
Both high  -> same-entity cluster, confident.
Disagree   -> steward review in the MADANU court.
Both low   -> genuinely distinct entities; left alone.
Thresholds are EAV attributes, tunable per domain.

## Clause 3 — Canonical Minting
A confirmed cluster enters the GL-TPL-001 minting rite.
The steward names and approves the CANONICAL SHAPE
Template — the unified shape DMBOK's Reference & Master
Data discipline demands — with each department's
divergent shape linked to it by CrossTribe-KAKI mapping
particles (field-level correspondence, versioned,
sealed). The canonical is a full KAKI particle in
EnkiMDB · 7006, HeptaScript-queryable like all law.

## Clause 4 — Enforcement Is the Contract
From approval forward, the canonical IS the contract:
- The DataStructure Station validates every future
  ingest against it; conformance passes silently.
- Divergence coalesces into a ticket (GL-TKT-001,
  one cause one ticket) and the steward decrees the
  mapping or the REWORK in MADANU (GL-DST-003 —
  appended, reasoned, sealed).
- DMBOK-derived unification policies live as rules in
  AkkadiRulesEngine; enforcement is policy execution,
  not persuasion.

## Clause 5 — Drift Watch
The survey recurs on schedule (Shakkanakku-governed).
Each department carries a DIVERGENCE METRIC — distance
of its live shapes from canonical — trended over time
and inscribed into Shakkanakku's Ed25519-sealed
stakeholder reports. Unification ceases to be a meeting
argument and becomes a signed number falling to zero.
