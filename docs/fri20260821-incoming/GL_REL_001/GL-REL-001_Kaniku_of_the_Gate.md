## Preamble

The Ecosystem is one body of law, one corpus of engines, one unbroken lineage of
playbooks. A body half-shown is a body misread. Until the internal processes of
v4.0 stand complete and testify for themselves, nothing of the body crosses the
outer boundary. This tablet fixes that decision as law, so that it binds not by
mood but by seal.

---

## §1 — Name and Standing

This law is named **Kanīku of the Gate**. It governs all acts of open-sourcing,
source disclosure, or public release of any artifact of BahyWay.Ecosystem v4.0.
It stands in the GL-series and is append-only: it may be superseded by a later
tablet, never edited.

## §2 — The SUSA Boundary

Open-sourcing is a **SUSA act**: a crossing of the outward gateway. Artifacts
covered by this law include, without exception:

- source code of any Engine, crate, or module (SilaEngine, IsimudEngine, the
  EnkiDB seven, and all siblings);
- GL-series tablets and the GX naming registry;
- PB-series playbooks;
- Šala HTML prototypes;
- schemas, byte layouts, HeptaScript constructs, and the KAKI specification;
- internal design documents, calculi, and pattern archives (Borsippa).

To publish any of these — repository, gist, snippet, attachment, screenshot of
source — is to cross the gate.

## §3 — The Gate Condition

The gate remains **sealed** until all v4.0 internal completion gates are green:

1. all numbered playbooks complete and tested (the governing law of build order);
2. all chartered engines proven against their own tablets;
3. the first-ingest rite (DataStructure Station, eight steps) demonstrated
   end-to-end on the Najaf 80,272-particle dataset;
4. the runtime target witnessed on the production court (one billion particles
   under one second, benchmark seal on the designated hardware);
5. the law corpus reconciled — no orphan tablets, no unnumbered fixes.

While any gate is not green, no artifact crosses. There is no partial credit.

## §4 — The Confirmation Act

Per CSR-08, the only confirmation act is the running of a numbered playbook.
Therefore: **no release may occur except by a numbered PB, run by DUB.SAR on
the Fedora bare-metal host.** No manual push, no ad-hoc repository creation, no
exception for "small" artifacts. A release that did not pass through a numbered
playbook did not lawfully occur.

## §5 — Ordering of Witnesses

The Two Witnesses doctrine applies to the Ecosystem itself:

- **First witness (NUZI, inward):** the internal processes, complete and green.
- **Second witness (SUSA, outward):** the world, receiving the release.

The second witness may not testify before the first. Internal proof precedes
external witness, always and without inversion.

## §6 — No Fragment Crossing

No fragment of the corpus crosses alone. A half-law invites misreading; a lone
crate invites forking of an unfinished paradigm; a leaked schema invites
imitation without understanding. The body crosses whole, or not at all.

## §7 — Permitted Declarations

The following are **declarations, not disclosures**, and remain lawful while
the gate is sealed:

- the Zenodo DOI whitepaper (paradigm establishment, no source);
- Benelux trademark filings (v3.5 boundary);
- the static declaration sites (bahyway.com, beemdm.com, heptascript.com),
  provided they carry no source, schema, or tablet text;
- conference talk and ACM Amsterdam discussion at the level of idea, never at
  the level of artifact.

A declaration states that the paradigm exists. A disclosure shows how it is
built. Only the former passes the sealed gate.

## §8 — Trigger and Successor

When, and only when, all gates of §3 stand green, this law does not release
anything by itself. It instead **fires a trigger**: the drafting of
**GL-REL-002 (the Release Rite)**, which shall define what crosses, in what
order, under what license, and by which numbered playbooks. Until GL-REL-002 is
sealed, the gate remains closed even with all gates green.

This mirrors the V5.0 Ḫibiltu Ledger deferral: a horizon deposited, not opened.
