# GL-TPL-002 — Living Shape & Drift Law
**Status:** SEALED (concept). Implementation queued behind PB-160.
**Owners:** MARDUK surface (witness), PDM Modeler lens
(repair), DataStructure Station (enforcement), Lamassu

Shakkanakku (survey cadence), MADANU (decrees).

## Clause 1 — Founding Shape vs Living Shape
The Shape Tablet sealed at first ingest (GL-TPL-001,
Station rite) describes the tribe at time zero: the
FOUNDING SHAPE, a covenant. The LIVING SHAPE is the
tribe's actual population at time t: its distribution
across houses, states, orbits, and dwell bands. Data
governance is the discipline of measuring and closing
the distance between the two. The Orbit View (BIGRING,
MARDUK) is the permanent witness of the living shape
and NEVER retires after onboarding.

## Clause 2 — The Three Faces of Drift
(a) AGING — cohorts whose dwell distributions stretch;
    the Aging Drift pattern operating at tribe scale.
(b) CAMPING — persistent sub-populations holding in
    EnkiSDB / EnkiODB that the founding shape never
    predicted.
(c) REJECTION POOLING — EnkiQDB accumulating a stable
    colony of refused matter.
All three change the tribe's effective shape and
pattern without a single schema edit.

## Clause 3 — Detection: Two Instruments, One Verdict
(a) TOPOLOGICAL — Lamassu computes persistent homology
    on the LIVE POPULATION: a founding beta_0 = 1 tribe
    showing beta_0 = 3 at runtime has visibly stranded
    islands. The same mathematics that judged the
    schema at Gate G4 judges the population in flight.
(b) BEHAVIORAL — story-shingle Jaccard between current
    cohort behavior and the founding pattern's shingle
    set; decayed match confidence means the pattern no
    longer describes its own tribe. The GL-MDM-001
    divergence metric extends per-tribe over time.
Cadence: Shakkanakku-scheduled surveys, plus event
triggers (ticket storms per GL-TKT-001, dwell alarms).

## Clause 4 — Response: the Steward's Loop
Drift witnessed in MARDUK -> cohort sent to the bench ->
the steward AMENDS the pattern or MINTS a new one
(GL-TPL-001 rite) -> authorized stakeholders open the
canonical in PDM and repair it against the new facts
(relations the pipeline taught, constraints, pattern
bindings) -> Gate G4 re-proves (Z3 · Lean4 · Algebra) ->
the SLA layer accepts the new canonical -> the Station
enforces it. EnkiDW mining findings enter the same door.

## Clause 5 — Versioned Canonicals (append, never update)
A canonical tablet is NEVER updated. A new version is
APPENDED as its own KAKI particle with a lineage link to
its predecessor — GL-DST-003 applied to tablets. The
contract's full history is queryable; a client can
always ask WHY their canonical changed and read every
version, every G4 certificate, every steward's WHY note.

## Clause 6 — Apsu as Completion Criterion
The stakeholders' work on a tribe continues until the
living shape converges onto its canonical and every
living particle's latest state is GOLDEN in EnkiDB ·
7004 (EnkiDW serving projections). The Apsu meter is
the PROGRESS BAR OF THE ENGAGEMENT — the contractual
completion criterion rendered as the golden Deep
(GL-DST-003 Clause 5), visible to client and steward
alike. Reaching Apsu is the deliverable.
