# Tablet of Asalluhi — Charter of the AsalluhiEngine
## GL-FOR-001 (candidate) · Forensics Domain · DRAFT — unsealed, awaiting Architect run & CSR-08 confirmation

---

## I. Identity

**Logical name (god layer, NL-001):** AsalluhiEngine
**Physical name (Ḫubullu gloss, GL-NAM-002):** Data Forensic Analysis Engine (DFA)
**Sector:** Forensics & Diagnosis · guardian of the BlackBox threshold (EnkiQDB, port 7003)
**Era of founding:** Zagesi · proposed 2026-08-10
**Naming rationale:** Asalluhi, son of Enki, god of incantation and exorcism — the one invoked when a demon must be *named before it can be expelled*. His portfolio is identification-before-expulsion, which is precisely the duty of the BlackBox: a dirty particle cannot merely be stored; it must be identified. As Enki's son he stands rightly at the threshold of the EnkiDB station family, guarding his father's houses.

The names Āšipu and Asû are deliberately **not** spent on the engine shell. They are the two professions of Mesopotamian medicine — the diagnostician of causes and the empirical physician of symptoms — and they name the engine's two internal calculi, where their meaning does real work.

---

## II. Mandate

AsalluhiEngine is the sole authority for answering three questions about any particle or cluster whose state has fallen UNKNOWN:

1. **What is it?** — identification of the sickness class (corrupt, malware, aged decay, or a class yet unnamed).
2. **Why is it?** — proof of the root cause beneath the symptom, with two independent witnesses.
3. **What removes it?** — prescription of the cause-level remedy as a numbered playbook, never a manual act.

The engine enforces the Analysis-to-Solution Law (DETECT → PROVE → PREDICT → PRESCRIBE) as a hard gate: no remediation decree may execute against a spot whose cause is unproven without an explicit, journalled symptom-only warning, and every symptom-only cure carries a recorded relapse prediction. The engine exists because a cure aimed at symptoms leaves the deeper degradation eating particles with the same pattern.

## III. The Two Calculi

### Āšipu Calculus — the diagnostician of causes (PROVE · PREDICT)

The āšipu determines which agent sent the illness. Members:

- **Evidence correlation rite** — lineage sweep across template identity, ingest batch, Riksu cross-tribe references, and PASHIRU colour history; dominant-share attribution per origin.
- **Root-cause taxonomy (C-xx registry)** — founding entries: C-01 SOURCE_FEED_POISON, C-02 TEMPLATE_DEFECT, C-03 PASHIRU_CALIBRATION, C-04 RIKSU_SEVERANCE, C-05 RENEWAL_RITE_HALTED. The registry is open; new causes are minted only through this engine.
- **Two-witness confirmation** — no cause is declared proven unless membrane-flux evidence and LamassuEngine topological evidence (vineyard instability, β₁ behaviour) independently agree, in the Shakkanakku two-instrument spirit.
- **Confidence functional** — diagnostic confidence expressed openly; a diagnosis below threshold remains a candidate, never a verdict.
- **Relapse projection** — predicted recurrence risk per remediation path (symptom-only vs cause-level), with quota and cost consequences, delivered to the steward court before any decree.
- **Prescription minting** — the proven cause binds to exactly one cause-level playbook; prescribing the wrong playbook for a proven cause is refused by the engine itself.

### Asû Calculus — the empirical physician of symptoms (DETECT · triage)

The asû reads wounds and applies remedies. Members:

- **Symptom taxonomy (R-xx registry)** — founding entries: R-01 CHECKSUM_DRIFT, R-02 EAV_MANDATORY_MISSING, R-03 HUE_VARIANCE, R-04 RIKSU_ORPHAN, R-05 STALE_TEMPORAL.
- **BlackBox identification rites** — the three founding identification classes with their forensic procedures: CORRUPT (CRC-16/CCITT forensics + NĀRU journal replay), MALWARE (Nisaba deep signature scan + sandboxed detonation), AGED/DECAY (lineage audit + GL-TPL-002 renewal adjudication). A clean particle bypasses the BlackBox at zero cost; identification is charged only to the dirty.
- **Fuzzy triage court** — membership ranges over membrane trill, unknown fraction, and density; rule firing; centroid defuzzification to the WATCH / ADVISE / QUARANTINE verdict ladder.
- **Particle testimony** — per-particle story assembly (mint, degradation events, crossings, symptom, and — once the āšipu has spoken — the named cause), delivered to StoryEngine under GL-STY-001.

**The doctrinal asymmetry is preserved in code:** the Asû Calculus may run alone (triage is always lawful); the Āšipu Calculus gates all *permanent* remediation. An asû without an āšipu produces uneasy calm, and the engine says so.

## IV. Boundaries

- **LamassuEngine** — remains the TDA orchestrator and second witness. Asalluhi consumes its persistence evidence; he never computes topology himself. Medium cadence, never per-particle, unchanged.
- **Nisaba** — remains the sovereign security-interface detector. Her malware signatures inform the Asû identification rite; verdicts of guilt belong to Asalluhi, delivery of alerts to Kittu.
- **Madanu Court (GL-DST-003)** — remains the adjudicator of contested classifications and change-order decrees. Asalluhi supplies evidence and prescriptions; Madanu decrees.
- **EnkiQDB (7003)** — remains the quarantine database, evidence-grade and prune-exempt under open PARZU investigations. Asalluhi is its threshold guardian, not its owner; storage law is unchanged.
- **ShalaEngine / Šala prototypes** — stakeholder education surfaces may *render* Asalluhi's rites (as the Tremor Watch does); they hold no authority.
- **GeoEngine** — remains the single mathematical truth source; any geometry Asalluhi needs, he asks for.
- **Z3** — design-time only, as ever; no solver ships in the engine binary.

## V. Invariants

1. Pure Rust; no runtime foreign solvers; KAKI v4.0 byte layout untouched and never reinterpreted.
2. Every verdict, diagnosis, prescription, and refusal is journalled append-only to NĀRU under Ed25519 seal.
3. PARZU role 0x01 advisories are inputs to this engine; it may recommend, never silently mutate, a particle's role.
4. No remediation without a numbered playbook; the engine emits playbook drafts, the Architect runs them (CSR-08).
5. Every sealed Akkadian term this engine mints carries its plain-language gloss at sealing time (Ḫubullu Law).
6. Relapse events are first-class journalled facts, billed and reputation-scored; the engine never hides a failed cure.
