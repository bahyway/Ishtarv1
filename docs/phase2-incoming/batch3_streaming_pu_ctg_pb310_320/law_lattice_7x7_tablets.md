## TABLET I — GL-LEX-001 "URRA" 𒄯𒊏 — The Lattice & Concordance Law

*Named for Urra=hubullu 𒄯𒊏 𒄷𒇧𒈝, the great Mesopotamian lexical list tradition: the tablets that cataloged all things without rewriting any of them.*

### Preamble

The laws of the ecosystem, having grown past forty tablets as a flat registry, are hereby constituted as a **7×7 lattice**: seven Major Laws, each a nucleus, each holding seven Derivative slots. Forty-nine addresses in total. The lattice echoes the sevens of the ecosystem: seven Apkallu, seven EnkiDB types, seven gates, seven lenses, seven dimensions of Hepta Space.

### Clause U-1 — Laws Are Particles (The Nippur Clause)

Every law of the ecosystem is itself a particle. The laws together form the **Law Tribe**, whose structure is named **NIPPUR** — the city of Enlil, where kingship and law were legitimated (structure named for a city, per NL-001).

- Each law-particle occupies a **unique real-valued position in 7D Hepta Space**, per the Hepta Space Uniqueness Law — which therefore governs itself. This self-reference is lawful and intended.
- Each law-particle orbits exactly one of the seven Major nuclei.
- The sealing of a law is a KAKI event: append-only, immutable, timestamped.
- Amendments (as with NL-001 A1/A2) are new particles in lineage with their parent, never edits to the parent.

### Clause U-3 — The Concordance Doctrine (No Tablet Is Renumbered)

Sealed tablets are immutable. **No existing law ID is ever renumbered, rewritten, or retired.** The lattice is an *overlay*:

- The original law ID (e.g. GL-DST-003, ML-001) is the **NUZI address** — inward, archival, lineage-bearing, valid forever.
- The lattice coordinate (Major.Derivative, e.g. 5.1) is the **SUSA address** — outward-facing, the gateway name by which the law is cited going forward.
- The Concordance (Clause U-6) is the mapping between the two. Like Girsu's archive: the catalog grows; the tablets do not change.

### Clause U-4 — Derivative Slots and Reserve Capacity

Each Major holds exactly seven Derivative slots (M.1 through M.7). Reserve capacity for future laws lives **inside each Major**, not in a global pool. A new law must declare its nucleus before drafting; if no slot remains under its Major, the Architect must either amend an existing derivative (new particle in lineage) or petition to split the Major — a constitutional act requiring its own tablet.

### Clause U-5 — Hepta Space Coordinates of Law-Particles (Provisional Mapping)

The seven dimensions of a law-particle's position are proposed as:

1. Major index (1–7)
2. Derivative index (1–7)
3. Seal date (epoch-normalized)
4. Lineage depth (0 for founding laws, +1 per amendment generation)
5. Playbook coupling (count of playbooks citing the law, log-scaled)
6. Proof weight (0 = declarative, 1 = Z3/Lean4-certified at design time)
7. Reserved (the Void is Sacred)

This mapping is provisional; dimensions 3–7 may be re-legislated without moving any law's lattice coordinate.

## TABLET II — GL-FLW-001 "PALGU" — The Continuous Ingest Law
### NIPPUR 5.1 · *palgu: the irrigation canal — water that arrives forever, by design*

### Clause P-1 — The Rite Proves the Contract, Not the Particle

The eight-step DataStructure Station rite (Landing→Safety→Inference→Arsenal match→Stakeholder PDM→Gate G4→SLA dual seal→ETL to GOLDEN) is a **design-time ceremony performed once per stream contract**, not per particle. Gate G4's Z3/Lean4 proofs certify the *contract*: schema, tolerances, event-time semantics, backpressure bounds. Z3 remains design-time only, per standing law.

### Clause P-2 — The Runtime Conformance Check

Each arriving stream particle passes only a lightweight structural conformance check against the sealed contract at the write node (192.168.122.101). The check must be constant-cost per particle and must never invoke a prover. A conforming particle receives its KAKI and enters the living tribe under GL-TPL-002 semantics. Sampling depth of the conformance check is a sealed contract parameter.

### Clause P-3 — Contract Immutability and Succession

A stream contract is a sealed particle. Any change — schema drift, tolerance revision, new attribute — is a **new contract requiring a new full rite**. The old contract closes with a NUZI lineage link to its successor; particles already ingested are never re-judged.

### Clause P-4 — Nonconformance

A particle failing conformance is never silently dropped. It is shed to the NUZI archival ledger with a PARZU-class advisory KAKI, visible to NuskuEngine.

## TABLET III — GL-FLW-002 "ADANNU" — The Event-Time Law
### NIPPUR 5.2 · *adannu: the appointed time — when a thing was fated, not when it was reported*

### Clause A-1 — The Two Times

Every stream particle carries two timestamps as EAV Mandatory Attributes:

- **adannu** (event time): when the event occurred in the world.
- **kašādu** (arrival time): when the particle crossed the gate at the write node.

Both are real-valued and participate in Hepta Space positioning. Neither may be inferred from the other.

### Clause A-2 — The Watermark

For each stream, the read node (192.168.122.107) maintains a **watermark**: the event-time frontier behind which the projection is declared *settled*. The watermark advancement rule (e.g. `max(adannu observed) − sealed lag bound`) is a contract parameter proven at Gate G4.

### Clause A-3 — Settlement Semantics

An Orbit View, Shakkanakku survey, or any DubSar lens rendered over event time must display its watermark. Regions beyond the watermark render as *unsettled* — visibly provisional, never presented as truth. Truth Before Beauty applies: an unsettled region may be beautiful, but it must confess.

### Clause A-4 — Utuabzu Patronage

Time as Scrubbable Axis governs replay: any settled interval may be scrubbed and re-projected deterministically from the KAKI ledger. Determinism of replay is a provable Gate G4 obligation.

## TABLET IV — GL-FLW-003 "ARKÛ" — The Late-Arrival Law
### NIPPUR 5.3 · *arkû: that which comes after — late, but not lawless*

### Clause K-1 — The Fourth Drift Class

GL-TPL-002's living tribe recognizes aging, camping, and reject drift. This law adds **arkû drift**: a particle whose adannu falls behind the watermark at kašādu. Arkû particles are lawful members of the tribe, not anomalies.

### Clause K-2 — The Tolerance Window

Each stream contract seals an **allowed lateness** bound. An arkû particle within tolerance is absorbed: the read node re-derives affected projections, and the affected settled region reopens with an explicit re-settlement KAKI marker. The write ledger is never rewritten — only projections move.

### Clause K-3 — Beyond Tolerance

An arkû particle beyond tolerance is shed to the NUZI archival ledger with full lineage — never dropped, never absorbed silently. A Gap-list procedure (in the manner of ML-001's engine recovery) may later petition for a supervised re-settlement rite, which is a design-time ceremony requiring Architect approval under CSR-08.

### Clause K-4 — Display of the Late

In Orbit View, arkû particles entering a re-settled region carry a visible drift signature distinct from aging/camping/reject, so that the tribe's history of lateness is legible in the theater.

## TABLET V — GL-FLW-004 "MĪLU" — The Flow & Backpressure Law
### NIPPUR 5.4 · *mīlu: the flood — when the river rises above the canal*

### Clause M-1 — The Capacity Covenant

The write node's ingest capacity is a sealed, measured number, tested against the standing runtime target: one billion particles under one second. Capacity is re-measured at every Zagesi-class release and recorded as a KAKI.

### Clause M-2 — The Three-Step Ladder

When ingest rate exceeds ENLIL's 7-index ring capacity, the write node responds in strict order:

1. **Buffer** — absorb within a sealed bound (a bounded canal basin; the bound is a Gate G4-proven contract parameter).
2. **Shed to NUZI** — beyond the buffer, particles flow to the NUZI archival ledger with a mīlu-marked KAKI, preserving full replayability. Shedding is loss of *immediacy*, never loss of *record*.
3. **Throttle upstream** — the SUSA gateway signals the source to slow. Throttling is the outward-facing act; NUZI shedding is the inward-facing act. The reserved names are placed exactly as doctrine demands.

Silent drop does not exist at any rung.

### Clause M-3 — Recovery and Replay

When the flood recedes, NUZI-shed particles replay through the standard conformance check (PALGU P-2) in adannu order. Replayed particles are arkû by construction and fall under GL-FLW-003.

### Clause M-4 — Watchfulness

NuskuEngine renders buffer depth, shed rate, and throttle state as first-class dashboard orbits. A mīlu event above a sealed severity emits a Massartu-pattern guardian alert (PB-177 arsenal).
