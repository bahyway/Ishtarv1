## The Six Clauses  *(verbatim)*

1. A topic is a logical nucleus; documents orbit it at a radius set by
   citation gravity. Exchange the central exemplar and the topic
   persists (PH-002).
2. Edges are citations. The Truth Before Beauty discipline is the KG's
   data source — curated at writing time, never scraped afterward.
3. Bridges are CrossTribe. A tablet whose citations span tribes is a
   bridge and carries kaki_type 0x03; bridges are the most valuable
   navigation results, not noise.
4. Drift is chronicled. Orbital migration across eras is a StoryEngine
   fact; a topic's history is replayable.
5. Gaps are holes. Persistent homology over the citation complex
   surfaces cited-but-missing tablets as documentation debt (Lamassu
   wiring post-gate).
6. Assignment is advisory. NINSUN proposes tribe membership; the
   Architect ratifies; nothing auto-seals.

## Orbital Mechanics (v1, sealed)  *(verbatim)*

    radius(d, T) = 1 / (1 + cites(d, T))     — more citations into
        tribe T pull document d closer to its nucleus.
    affinity(d, T) = cites(d,T) / total_cites(d)
    home tribe = argmax affinity; bridge if second-best
        affinity >= 0.30 (threshold tunable, sealed default).
    related(d) = nearest neighbors by |radius difference| within the
        home tribe, plus all bridges touching it.
