Q&A-2026-08-10 · session “Unified Algebra Theorem as BahyWay Algebra”

DUB.SAR 𒁾 — the Architect asks

“We need to structure the EAV of EnkiDDB so it shows not only documents, versions and release dependencies, but also the related Akkadian terms — agents, gates, engines, datasets — all as Particles. What are the best practices for an EAV schema that does not only depend on the Graph RAG, but builds real classification from the unique Categories and Domains of BahyWay Tribes and Particles?”

Claude answers

Classification lives in sealed EAV mandatory attributes; the Graph RAG is one derived lens over it — rebuildable by rite, never authoritative. Deterministic taxonomy first, statistical retrieval second.

Take “all are Particles” literally: every Akkadian name is minted as a Term particle with its own KAKI; a document never contains a mere string — it holds a cross-tribe relation to the term particle. The Ḫubullu gloss becomes an EAV attribute on the term itself.

Classify by independent facets, not a tree: doc_class, noun_class, role_class, arsenal_tier, domain, seal_state, era, gloss, lineage — closed domains, minted like tribes, never renamed.

A derived facet may propose; only a sealed facet may classify. Statistical clusters carry derived=true and advisory status — the reflex/cortex ladder applied to knowledge.

Relations use a closed verb set — DEFINES, USES, SEALS, SUPERSEDES, GLOSSES, DEPENDS_ON, INSTANTIATES — new domains cost new nouns, never new verbs.
