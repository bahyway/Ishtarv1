## 0 · Principle

Nothing in medicine is static, yet its labels are sacrosanct. "Heart," "lung,"
"brain," "stomach" are fixed names; what is known about their structure,
disease, contagion, and metrics changes daily. Therefore a medical domain is a
**sealed boundary around a permanently-unsealed interior**:

- the **label** is a Domain particle with fixed KAKI identity (sacrosanct name,
  orthography law — plain single words, no drift);
- the **interior** is a Living Shape (GL-VIZ-001) — zoomable, fly-through,
  aging, its dependencies walking, its membranes transparent — never a frozen
  schema.

A visualization that froze the interior would contradict medicine itself. The
domain name endures; the model within it is always open to the day's discovery.

  passable membrane.

## 2 · Daily Discovery = Continuous Shape Verdict

"Accommodating the daily discovery of new features or refinement of existing
metrics" is a *temporal* requirement: a medical domain's schema is never
finished. Each new finding enters as a discovered shape that may not match the
domain's current template — and **GL-VIZ-002 runs continuously** to classify it:

- new cardiac sub-structure never modeled → **Verdict 1** (new template).
- a metric we had wrong (e.g. a refined reference range) → **Verdict 2**
  (template defect; amend + re-seal, old version kept as scar).
- a dependency medicine only just uncovered (e.g. a newly found comorbidity
  link) → **Verdict 3** (incomplete shape; complete the definition).

Medicine's own advance becomes a stream of shape-verdicts refining the arsenal.
The anatomy is alive because the verdict rite never closes.

## 3 · The Refinement-Date Clause (medical honesty, binding)

In medicine, *"when did we last learn this?"* is itself a transparency metric.
Therefore, extending GL-VIZ-001's honesty coupling (D-1/D-2):

- **M-1 · Every membrane shows its refinement date + provenance.** No anatomical
  structure renders its detail without displaying when that detail was last
  refined and from which sealed evidence. A membrane without its refinement date
  is a beautiful lie.
- **M-2 · Morphology proposes; evidence proves (medical form).** The living
  model of a lung *proposes* what is known; the sealed clinical evidence and
  literature *prove* it. Clinicians and patients must never mistake the model's
  current detail for settled fact. Staleness (ε) and refinement age are shown as
  honestly as structure.
- **M-3 · Advisory-only inherited.** GL-MED-001 Namtila principle holds: the
  Living Anatomy informs; it never diagnoses or gates care. It is a map of
  knowledge, not a verdict on a patient.
