# GL-GOV-003 (candidate) — THE COMPRESSION GATE
## The control layer: nothing crosses from the simulation workshop into the stakeholder view without an authority signature — and it crosses wearing its true provenance
### BahyWay.Ecosystem v4.0 · binds GL-GOV-002 (Earned Assertion) · GL-GOV-001 (Submission) · GL-HS3-002 (ε) · the three-layer architecture · Status: DRAFT — pending CSR-08 by DUB.SAR 𒁾

---

## 0 · Why this layer exists

Three layers, three audiences:
1. **Discovery** (naṣāru + FCA) — *measured* structure. Derived, provable.
2. **Simulation** (Wolfram-style, seeded by discovery) — *imagined* structure.
   Generated; developer + authority only.
3. **Stakeholder view** (the Sankey / clean compression) — what a stakeholder
   reads and acts on.

**The danger:** the stakeholder view *compresses* — and compression strips the ε
whisper. A clean Sankey ribbon *asserts certainty* by its very form. A stakeholder
cannot tell a proven flow from an imagined one once both are drawn as clean
ribbons. A simulated connection that leaks into the stakeholder view **is an
imagination shown as a fact, invisibly.** The prettier and simpler the view, the
more total the deception, because the view's power comes precisely from hiding
complexity — and it hides the uncertainty along with it.

**The refusal (STOP):** *Do not use simulation as stakeholder visualization.*
Only structure the authority has admitted — with evidence and signature — may
cross, and it crosses wearing its true provenance.

---

## 1 · The Law

**No shape may cross from the Simulation layer into the Stakeholder view except
through the Compression Gate. The Gate admits a shape only if a member of the
High Authorized Group has (a) reviewed it, (b) admitted it with its evidence and
their signature, and (c) it crosses wearing its true provenance — recorded as an
admitted, witnessed, revisable ruling, never silently promoted to fact.
Discovered structure passes freely; simulated structure passes only signed;
unproven-and-unsigned structure does not pass.**

---

## 2 · What the signature does — and does not do

**The signature does NOT turn imagination into truth** (that would relocate the
oracle into the authority's pen). It turns an imagined shape into an
**accountable, attributable, reversible decision**: a named authority, at a named
rank, at a named time, stands behind admitting *this* shape *on this evidence*,
and can be held to it and can reverse it. An admitted-simulated shape is not
"a fact" — it is "a shape [signer] admitted on [date] on evidence [Y], revisable."
The stakeholder is told it is *admitted by a named authority*, not that it is
proven — a different and honest claim.

---

## 3 · The three crossing verdicts

| Origin | Verdict | Crosses as |
|---|---|---|
| **Discovered** (measured, FCA-derived) | passes freely | `derived` — solid ribbon |
| **Simulated + signed** (admitted, with evidence) | passes signed | `admitted` — solid, marked with signer + revisable tag |
| **Simulated + unsigned** | **STOP — does not pass** | remains in the workshop |
| **Unknown / high-ε** | not as fact | `candidate` — visibly provisional (dashed/lighter), never a solid ribbon |

**Default-closed for simulation.** Discovered structure is the only thing that
flows without a signature. Silence = not admitted.

---

## 4 · Provenance survives compression (anti-laundering)

Compression strips detail but **never** provenance. Every ribbon carries its
origin class — `derived` / `admitted` (with signer) / `candidate`. If a rendering
cannot carry at least the origin class, it **may not render the non-derived shape
at all.** No provenance channel → no crossing. (Binds GL-GOV-002 §4.)

---

## 5 · The Gate is a physical checkpoint, not a guideline
- The stakeholder-view renderer accepts input **only** from the Gate's output,
  never directly from the Simulation layer.
- The Gate emits a crossing token only against a valid signature record (signer,
  rank, evidence, timestamp) in the append-only NĀRU journal under Ed25519 seal.
- A shape without a token is **undrawable** in the stakeholder view — the STOP is
  enforced by the absence of a code path, not by a reviewer's memory.

---

## 6 · Honest scope
- **Guaranteed by the Gate:** no simulated shape reaches the stakeholder view
  without a signature record; no ribbon renders without a provenance class.
- **Not guaranteed:** the Gate cannot ensure the authority *signs wisely*. A
  signer can admit a bad shape. What the Gate guarantees is that the admission is
  **attributable and reversible** — always a name on it, always a way to undo it.
  The defence against a bad signature is witness, rank, and revisability — not
  infallibility. Stated, not hidden.
