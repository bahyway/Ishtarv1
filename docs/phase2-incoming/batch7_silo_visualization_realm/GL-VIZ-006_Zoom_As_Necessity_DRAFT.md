## 0 · The name

The Symbolic Visualization Programming Language of BahyWay is **BWVL**. Its
Akkadian name is **naṣāru** (𒌷 URU₃; root n-ṣ-r) — the verb **"to guard, watch,
protect, preserve, treasure."** BWVL is *the act of guarding-and-watching the
truth of the data*, made into a language.

**Why naṣāru fits, verified against the dictionaries (CAD / CDA):**
- The gloss is BWVL's whole soul: *to guard, protect, defend, safeguard; to
  watch, beware of, cherish, preserve/conserve, prize, treasure, be careful.*
  The instrument guards the true — the four-outcome honesty contract, the GOLDEN
  store, the particle carrying its own certainty, are all acts of *naṣāru*.
- **The star-watch resonance:** the attested idiom *maṣṣartu naṣāru* means "to
  stand guard / to be on sentry duty / **to observe / gaze at the stars**"
  (astronomy). *naṣāru* is the very verb Mesopotamian astronomers used for
  watching the night sky. For an instrument whose nature is watching a field of
  a billion star-like particles and guarding their truth, the word is both "to
  guard" and "to keep the star-watch" — the watchman of truth and the watcher of
  the cosmic field are one word.
- **Naming-law clean:** *naṣāru* is a **verb / common concept, not a deity** — so
  it does not collide with the "gods = engines" law (unlike the earlier
  candidates Mullilu/Mullissu, Ninimma, or Kulla, all deities). A language is
  rightly named by an act, not a god.

**Form chosen:** the **verb (the act)** — *naṣāru* — not the agent noun *nāṣiru*
("the guardian") nor the office *maṣṣartu* ("the watch/guard-duty"). BWVL is the
*act of guarding*, not a doer or a place.

**Orthography note:** transliterated **naṣāru** with emphatic **ṣ** (ṣade), not
plain "s"; citation form with mimation *naṣārum*; dictionary headword
*naṣāru(m)*. Per the ecosystem Orthography Clause, the runtime/codename form is
rendered as the unbroken plain-Latin single word **nasaru** where diacritics
cannot be carried, but the scholarly form **naṣāru** is the sealed name of
record.

## 1 · The Law (the capstone)

**The zoom is not ornamental. The Hubble-descent (zoom-in to the single
particle) and the bird's-eye (zoom-out to the whole field) are the EXISTENCE
CONDITION of BWVL. Without them, self-describing particles at billion-scale are
visible but unreachable — a locked wall. The zoom is the only bridge between the
billion and the one, and therefore it is a first-class law of the language, not
a camera convenience.**

---

## 2 · Why it is necessity, not nicety

Three facts, together, force the conclusion:

1. **The particle is the atom of meaning** (GL-VIZ-003). Each carries its tribe
   (hue), identity (shade-degree), state (brightness), certainty, and existence
   — readable in the dot itself (GL-VIZ-004). *This is the difference from Neo4j:
   the particle IS the meaning, not an address to a panel.*
2. **A billion particles are read as a FIELD, by looking** — tribes, densities,
   golden-clusters, voids — the bird's-eye. This is only possible because
   particles are self-describing (fact 1).
3. **No display can individually resolve a billion particles.** To reach ONE
   particle — its KAKI, its EAV, its links — you MUST descend through scale.

Therefore: self-describing particles + billion-scale + reachability-of-any-one
**cannot coexist without the zoom.** Remove the descent and BWVL collapses into
the Neo4j failure — everything visible, nothing reachable; a gorgeous prison.
The zoom is the load-bearing invention.

## 3 · The two motions (both first-class)

- **Bird's-eye (zoom-out / focus-fly):** read the WHOLE as a field. Enigmatic
  focus-camera flies to the region the eye chose. Reveals tribe-bands (colour),
  federation structure (CrossTribe-KAKI), density and void.
- **Hubble-descent (zoom-in):** dissolve field → region → cluster → the single
  particle, reaching its KAKI + EAV (GL-VIZ-003 dissolve). Continuous, not
  stepped modes.

Because everything is one substance (Particle Monism), the SAME descent works on
ANY element — a particle, an icon (VPL), a relation, a label, a tribe (BIGRING),
the federation itself. One gesture, all scales.
