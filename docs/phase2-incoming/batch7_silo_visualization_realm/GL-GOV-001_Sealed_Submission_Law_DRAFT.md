# GL-GOV-001 (candidate) — THE SEALED SUBMISSION LAW
## Nothing enters BahyWay.Ecosystem on a stakeholder's assertion alone; every correction passes a ranked authority and carries a witness
### BahyWay.Ecosystem v4.0 · binds GL-MEM-001 (Mašḫalu) · GL-FOR-001 (AsalluhiEngine) · the FCA correction layer · CSR-08 (Architect Sovereignty) · Status: DRAFT — pending CSR-08 by DUB.SAR 𒁾

---

## 0 · Why this law exists

The naṣāru instrument computes structure from data (FCA). But a computed shape
may not capture 100% of the real data structure — the formal context can be
incomplete, binary attributes lose nuance, and genuine higher-order relations
(among three or more entities) are invisible to a pairwise lattice. Stakeholders
must therefore be able to *correct* the shape — add or curve the missing edges
and relations.

**The danger this law seals:** if a stakeholder can freehand any edge they wish,
the instrument stops *guarding truth* and starts *laundering opinion*. A
hand-drawn relation would wear the authority of computation without the evidence
of it. This is the same failure mode the ecosystem has refused elsewhere
(GOLDEN ⟹ verified fact, never prediction; naṣāru is an instrument, not an
oracle). The correction layer must not reopen it.

---

## 1 · The Law

**Nothing in BahyWay.Ecosystem — no correction, no added edge, no asserted
relation, no minted pattern, no schema change — may be submitted by a
stakeholder and enter the ecosystem without the approval of the High Authorized
Group. A stakeholder may *propose*; only the ranked authority may *admit*. Every
admitted correction carries a witness recording who proposed it, who approved it,
and the evidence (counterexample or proof) on which it was admitted.**

---

## 2 · The High Authorized Group (the ranked authority)

Three ranks, in descending order of authority over *what the ecosystem holds as
true*:

| Rank | Role | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Architect** | highest rank | Final sovereign approval (CSR-08). May admit, reject, or seal any correction, pattern, or law. Nothing overrides the Architect. |
| **2 · Steward** | second rank | Governs data-quality corrections: admits or refuses proposed edges/relations, adjudicates counterexamples, oversees aging/decay and Namburbi rites. Escalates law-level or pattern-level changes to the Architect. |
| **3 · Administrator** | **last in the Visualization rank, first in the Automation & Infrastructure rank** | Governs the running of the machinery: playbooks, provisioning, pipelines, deployment, the HA/DR and infra rites. Holds no authority to admit a *truth* correction (that is the Steward's and Architect's), but holds first authority over *how* the ecosystem runs. |

**The two axes.** The Administrator is deliberately *last in visualization,
first in automation/infra* — the ranks are not a single ladder but two: authority
over **truth/meaning** (Architect › Steward › Administrator) and authority over
**operation/infrastructure** (Administrator › … ). A correction to *what is true*
climbs the truth axis; a change to *how it runs* climbs the operation axis. No
role may act outside its axis without escalation.

## 3 · The submission gate (guided, not freehand)

A stakeholder correction follows the **attribute-exploration discipline** (the
canonical FCA human-in-the-loop procedure), never free drawing:

1. **PROPOSE** — the stakeholder proposes a correction. If it is an added edge or
   relation, it is framed as a claim: *"these objects also share this relation"*
   or *"this implication should / should not hold."*
2. **WITNESS-REQUIRED** — the proposer must supply evidence:
   - to *add* a relation: the objects/records that ground it (the extent);
   - to *refute* a computed implication: a **counterexample** — a real object
     that has the premise but lacks the conclusion. (No counterexample, no
     refutation. This is what keeps the corrected lattice logically consistent
     with the data, not merely with belief.)
3. **RANKED APPROVAL** — the proposal is routed by axis:
   - a *truth* correction → **Steward** admits or refuses; law/pattern-level →
     escalates to the **Architect**;
   - an *operation/infra* change → **Administrator** admits within its axis.
4. **WITNESS SEALED** — on admission, an append-only NĀRU journal entry records
   proposer, approver, rank, evidence, and timestamp under Ed25519 seal. On
   refusal, the reason and (if any) the counterexample are recorded too.
5. **PROVENANCE TAG** — the admitted edge/relation is tagged **asserted**
   (expert-admitted with witness), distinct from **derived** (computed by FCA
   from data). The two never blur. A reader can always tell whether a relation
   was proved by the data or admitted by an authority.

**No step may be skipped.** A correction with no witness, or no ranked approval,
does not enter — it remains a proposal.

## 4 · Tool-role separation (kept clean, per prior doctrine)

The correction layer uses four tools, each with one job; none claims another's:
- **FCA** — *discovers* the lattice, implications, and derived metamodel from data.
- **Attribute exploration** — *corrects* the lattice through propose → confirm /
  refute-with-counterexample (the guided loop of this law).
- **Simplicial complexes** — *represent* the higher-order relations (among 3+
  entities) that a pairwise lattice cannot hold; an admitted higher-order
  relation becomes a simplex (triangle, tetrahedron…), not a fake edge.
- **Geometric Algebra** — *renders and computes* the geometry of admitted edges
  and curves (lines, incidences, subspaces as multivectors). It draws what the
  authority admitted; it does not discover or admit.
