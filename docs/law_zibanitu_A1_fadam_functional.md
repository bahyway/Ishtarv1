## §A1.1 — Definition (the Fadam Functionals)

Let 𝕋 be the sort of tribes and 𝚯 the sort of **sealed** templates (Zibānītu Calculus
§1). A functional

> **F : 𝕋 × 𝚯 → ℝ₊**

is a **Fadam functional** iff it satisfies the five Fadam axioms:

- **F1 — Anonymity.** F(σT ‖ Θ) = F(T ‖ Θ) for every permutation σ of particles.
  *The verdict reads the tribe, never the individual.*
- **F2 — Puhu Invariance.** F(R_φ T ‖ R_φ Θ) = F(T ‖ Θ) for every rotation R_φ.
  *The verdict is blind to orientation, awake only to deformation.*
- **F3 — Stability.** F is Lipschitz in T: there exists L < ∞ with
  │F(T ‖ Θ) − F(T′ ‖ Θ)│ ≤ L · d(T, T′) for the tribe metric d (mean angular + radial
  + count displacement). *Verdicts cannot move faster than the world that moves them.*
- **F4 — The Honest Floor.** There is a declared uncertainty ε : 𝕋 → ℝ₊, ε > 0, with
  F(T ‖ Θ) ≥ ε(T) for all T — **including T = Θ**. *No Fadam functional can report a
  deficit of zero; claiming certainty is forbidden by type, not by discipline.*
- **F5 — The Sealed Judge.** dom(F) admits only Θ ∈ sealed templates. On drafts only
  the rehearsal shadow F̃ exists, and no DETECT event is derivable from F̃.
  *An unsealed assumption may rehearse; it may never judge.*

## §A1.2 — The Fadam Inequality

> **ε(T) ≤ F(T ‖ Θ) ≤ ε(T) + L · d(T, Θ)**

side is humility (F3): no verdict exceeding what the measured distance to the declared
rhythm can justify. **A system whose judgments live inside the Fadam Inequality can be
persuasive and accountable at once** — the formal statement of "we made truth visible to
everyone, and made the visibility itself accountable."
