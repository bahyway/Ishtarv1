SC+GA PROBE — Graveyard Rat Detection ☠ (Demo)

An ironic demonstration of Simplicial Complex & Geometric Algebra applied to the ancient question: "where are the rats?" 19 rats modelled as BahyWay particles across 4 graveyard sectors (orbits). The PROBE builds a Vietoris-Rips complex at threshold epsilon, computes Betti numbers β₀ (colonies) and β₁ (circuit loops), then navigates inward via Cl(2,0) rotors.
Adjust ε to watch topology change in real time — same engine powers NajafEngine crowd-path analysis and WPDEngine pipeline loop detection.
