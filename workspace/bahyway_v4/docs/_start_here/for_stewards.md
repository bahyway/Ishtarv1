## KAKI — What Gives a Particle Its Identity

Every particle you steward has a KAKI — its sovereign identity seal, from
the Akkadian *kaku* (𒋼𒁀): a cylinder seal pressed into clay, impossible
to forge. **KAKI (Knowledge–Akkadian–Keyword–Identity)** means:

- The particle *knows* structured facts about a real-world entity
- Its identity is *sealed at birth* and cannot change, even as its quality
  score degrades from GEM to DEAD
- Its state is changed by appending new Event-Kakis — never by modifying
  the original KAKI bytes
- Two particles representing the same real-world entity are resolved
  deterministically through the sovereign index structure — not by
  probabilistic name or address matching

As a steward, you read KAKI PKs to audit lineage, resolve gaps in the
`seq_counter` (`κ[8..11]`), and confirm that no particle has been removed
from the Journal. A gap in the sequence is the system's way of telling
you something happened that should not have.
