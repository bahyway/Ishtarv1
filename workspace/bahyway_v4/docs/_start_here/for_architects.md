## KAKI — What You Are Building On

**KAKI (Knowledge–Akkadian–Keyword–Identity)** is the sovereign identity
primitive at the centre of every architectural decision in BahyWay v4.0.

From the Akkadian *kaku* (𒋼𒁀) — a cylinder seal pressed into clay,
impossible to forge. BahyWay's KAKI is a 16-byte sovereign seal: minted
once, immutable for the particle's lifetime, encoding structural identity
only (never assessments). Entity resolution is deterministic and auditable
— no ML embedding, no probabilistic matching, no external model.
